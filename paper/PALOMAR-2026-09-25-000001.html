Absence of bounded-orbit wandering domains

Nikolai Prochorov, Lasse Rempe, James Waterman

TL;DR

Abstract
We prove that transcendental entire functions do not have bounded-orbit wandering domains. This answers a major open question in the field. The proof builds upon Zihao Ye's new proof of Sullivan's No Wandering Domains theorem, and was obtained with assistance by generative AI. We also formalise a stronger theorem, which shows that locally defined holomorphic functions do not have orbits of simply connected wandering domains. The formalisation includes a number of classical results that were implemented as auxiliary results, including results concerning the hyperbolic metric for a finitely punctured sphere, and its area.

Problem

A major open question in complex dynamics asks whether transcendental entire functions can have bounded-orbit wandering domains.

Approach

The proof builds on Zihao Ye's new proof of Sullivan's No Wandering Domains theorem and was obtained with assistance from generative AI. A stronger theorem, for locally defined holomorphic functions and orbits of simply connected wandering domains, is formalised. The formalisation includes classical auxiliary results, among them results on the hyperbolic metric of a finitely punctured sphere and its area.
