Abstract
A Lean 4 / Mathlib formalization of the whole of P. Corvaja and U. Zannier, On the rational approximations to the powers of an algebraic number: solution of two problems of Mahler and Mendès France (Acta Math. 193 (2004)), built on the repository's libraries ArithmeticHeights and DiophantineApproximation, whose Subspace Theorem it uses. The library CorvajaZannier2004 is sorry-free and within Lean's three standard axioms. It proves the Main Theorem (finitely many non-pseudo-Pisot δqu that are very close to integers, u in a finitely generated group of algebraic numbers), Theorem 1 (Mahler's question: if ‖αⁿ‖ < lⁿ infinitely often, some power of α is a Pisot number), Lemmas 1–5, Theorem 2 (Mendès France's question: the period of the continued fraction of αⁿ tends to infinity unless α is a unit or a square root of a rational, and is bounded for units), and the Appendix (the word "algebraic" cannot be dropped from Theorem 1). Eleven statements are compared.

Problem

Corvaja and Zannier (Acta Math. 2004) answered two problems, one of Mahler and one of Mendès France, about rational approximations to powers of an algebraic number. The goal is a complete machine-checked formalization of that paper.

Approach

The library CorvajaZannier2004 is built in Lean 4 with Mathlib on top of the repository's ArithmeticHeights and DiophantineApproximation libraries. It uses the Subspace Theorem supplied by those libraries. The Main Theorem, Theorems 1 and 2, Lemmas 1–5, and the Appendix are all formalized.

Results

The library is sorry-free and depends only on Lean's three standard axioms. It covers Mahler's question (Pisot powers), Mendès France's question on continued-fraction periods of αⁿ, and the Appendix showing that the word "algebraic" cannot be dropped from Theorem 1. Eleven formal statements are compared against the paper's statements.
