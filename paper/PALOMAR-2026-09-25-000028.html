Problem

The Gibbard-Satterthwaite theorem states that, with at least three alternatives, every onto and strategy-proof social choice function is dictatorial. The entry gives a machine-checked proof of this result for finite electorates and finite sets of alternatives.

Approach

The Lean formalization builds a social welfare function from a given strategy-proof social choice function. It then derives the result by reducing it to Arrow's impossibility theorem.

Results

A complete formal proof shows that onto, strategy-proof social choice functions on at least three alternatives are dictatorial. The Palomar registry machine-checked the proofs.
