Global asymptotic stability of homogeneous polynomial vector fields is undecidable

Ivan O. Shevchenko, Jun Liu, Xinzhi Liu

Abstract
We study whether global asymptotic stability (GAS) of the origin is decidable for homogeneous polynomial vector fields with rational coefficients. Our main technical contribution is showing that, for such vector fields of odd degree $d \geq 3$ in every sufficiently large dimension $n$, the problem of deciding GAS of the origin is many-one complete for the class of recursively enumerable sets (and hence undecidable). Using the tools we have built up, we also improve on some recent work by proving a similar result for Lyapunov stability instead of GAS. The proof of our main contribution splits naturally into two parts: - In the first part we give, for every fixed dimension $n$ and odd degree $2k+1$, a computable map which sends a homogeneous polynomial vector field of degree $2k+1$ on $\mathbb{R}^n$ to a homogeneous polynomial vector field of degree three on $\mathbb{R}^m$, where $m=\binom{n+k-1}{k}$, and preserves both GAS and Lyapunov stability. As a consequence, the problems of deciding whether the origin is GAS or Lyapunov stable for rational-coefficient homogeneous polynomial vector fields of any degree, of any fixed odd degree $d \geq 3$, and of degree three are pairwise many-one equivalent. - In the second part we prove that there is a fixed dimension $n_*$ and a fixed odd degree $d_*$ such that the set of rational-coefficient homogeneous polynomial vector fields on $\mathbb{R}^{n_*}$ of degree $d_*$ whose origin is GAS is many-one complete. This is done by "transferring" many-one completeness between several different problems, starting from the solvability of a Diophantine equation and eventually arriving at our desired GAS decision problem.
Problem

The question is whether global asymptotic stability (GAS) or Lyapunov stability of the origin is decidable for homogeneous polynomial vector fields with rational coefficients.

Approach

A computable Veronese-type cubic lift maps odd-degree homogeneous fields to cubic fields while preserving GAS and Lyapunov stability. Many-one completeness is then transferred along a chain of problems: from Diophantine solvability (MRDP), through Fourier-series constant terms and invariant-measure drift, to GAS of an explicit vector field. The results were generated with AI assistance and formalized in Lean 4 using Mathlib, and a statement-to-Lean correspondence and an axiom audit are reported.

Results

For odd degree d >= 3 in all sufficiently large dimensions, deciding GAS of the origin is many-one complete for r.e. sets, and hence undecidable. An analogous hardness result is proved for Lyapunov stability. The GAS problems for any degree, any fixed odd degree, and degree three are pairwise many-one equivalent.
