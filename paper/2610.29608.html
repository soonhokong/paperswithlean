Residue Sums Modulo Products of Two Distinct Odd Primes

Haobo Ma, Mircea Merca, Wenlin Zhang

math.NT Oct 21, 2026 · v1
formalization number-theory
Read PDF arXiv abstract Code
TL;DR
Lean 4 formalizations, with a pinned public repository, verify a counterexample to Merca's Conjecture 1 and prove his lifted prime-modulus residue-sum Conjecture 2.
Abstract
Let $S_m(a)$ denote the sum of the least positive residues in one multiplicative period of $a$ modulo $m$, of length $k$. We study the equality $S_m(a)=mk/2$ for $m=pq$, where $p,q$ are distinct odd primes and $k$ is even. Complementary pairing settles subgroups containing $-1$; the remaining cases depend on their embedding in the two local unit groups. At index four, we exclude mixed cyclic subgroups with biquadratic annihilator and classify every eligible imprimitive quartic kernel using two odd integer moments at its prime conductor. For each fixed conductor, the proportion of admissible second primes giving equality is $1/4$ or $1/2$; full coset balance has proportion $1/4$. For primitive quartic characters, classical triangular character-sum identities yield explicit short-sum criteria, and the relative class-number formula gives an obstruction to a centered coset. At index six, we give structural counts and construct an infinite family modulo $19q$ in which nonzero sextic and quadratic moments cancel. These applications use a standard odd-character expansion, which also recovers the known index-two balance classification and determines the sign of its deviation. A reproducible census for $pq\le10000$ counts $75\,090$ eligible cyclic subgroups and identifies $101$ mixed index-four subgroups whose own sum is centered although some coset sums are not.
Problem

The paper asks when the sum of least positive residues over one multiplicative period of a modulo m=pq equals mk/2, for distinct odd primes p, q and even order k. This addresses a conjecture of Merca that a counterexample refutes.

Approach

An odd-character expansion expresses the deviation from mk/2 through first moments of odd characters in the annihilator of the subgroup. Class-number formulas and character-sum identities then analyze subgroups of index two, four and six. The counterexample (2,15) to Merca's Conjecture 1 and the lifted prime-modulus identity (Conjecture 2) are formalized in Lean 4. A Python census covers all pq ≤ 10000.

Results

Mixed index-four subgroups with biquadratic annihilator are never balanced. Imprimitive quartic kernels are fully classified, with fixed-conductor proportions of 1/4 or 1/2. An infinite index-six family modulo 19q is constructed. The census finds 75,090 eligible subgroups and 101 mixed index-four subgroups that are centered while some of their cosets are not.
