Abstract
A Lean formalization of the qualitative well-definedness of the nonnegativity Sturm bound A(k) for real level-one modular forms. For every weight k at least 12 and divisible by four, the formalization proves that a finite initial segment detects coefficientwise nonnegativity and that its optimal endpoint is attained as the greatest possible first negative Fourier-coefficient index.

Problem

For real level-one modular forms, one wants a finite cutoff A(k) such that nonnegativity of the first Fourier coefficients implies nonnegativity of all of them. The qualitative well-definedness of this nonnegativity Sturm bound needs a rigorous justification.

Approach

The result is formalized in Lean for every weight k at least 12 and divisible by four. The formalization shows that some finite initial segment of Fourier coefficients detects coefficientwise nonnegativity. It then characterizes the optimal endpoint of that segment.

Results

The formalization proves that a finite detecting segment exists and that its optimal endpoint equals the greatest possible index of a first negative Fourier coefficient. The proofs were machine-checked by the Palomar registry.
