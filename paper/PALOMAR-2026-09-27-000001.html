Uniform Minor-Arc Cancellation for Primes at the X^{2/15+ε} Scale

Conor Grogan

TL;DR
Formalizes in Lean 4 a uniform minor-arc bound for prime exponential sums at the X^(2/15+ε) scale, including a proof of the Guth–Maynard large-value estimate.
Abstract
We prove uniform cancellation in prime-weighted exponential sums at the 2/15 scale, improving the 8/33 local Fourier scale in the Matomäki–Radziwiłł–Tao machinery for averaged prime-pair correlations. On the minor-arc part of every centered arc of radius 1/(2H), the squared von Mangoldt exponential sum over X < n <= 2X has integral at most C X (log X)^(-A), for every A > 0 and epsilon > 0, once H >= X^(2/15+epsilon) and X is large enough. The center may lie anywhere on the circle. The same cutoffs and constants work for every center and are chosen before X and H. This gives uniform minor-arc cancellation at the 2/15 threshold. Since 2/15 < 8/33, the admissible H is smaller, so the frequency windows of length 1/H are wider and the shift-averaging intervals are shorter. The constants C and X0 are ineffective because the proof uses Siegel's theorem. The development also contains an internal proof of the epsilon-form of Guth–Maynard's large-value estimate for Dirichlet polynomials. The recorded 20 September Lean 4.30 kernel replays checked 100,596 declarations for MAP and 75,874 for Guth–Maynard, using only standard axioms. The formal statement is AllCenterMAP.map_two_fifteenths, manuscript Theorem 1.1.

Problem

Averaged prime-pair correlations in the Matomäki–Radziwiłł–Tao approach rely on minor-arc cancellation for von Mangoldt exponential sums over local Fourier windows. The previously available scale was H ≥ X^(8/33). The goal is uniform cancellation at the smaller scale H ≥ X^(2/15+ε).

Approach

The authors bound the integral of the squared von Mangoldt exponential sum over X < n ≤ 2X on the minor-arc part of every centered arc of radius 1/(2H). The cutoffs and constants are fixed before X and H and hold for every center on the circle. The argument uses Siegel's theorem, so the constants are ineffective. It also includes an internal proof of the ε-form of the Guth–Maynard large-value estimate for Dirichlet polynomials, and the whole development is formalized in Lean 4.

Results

The minor-arc integral is at most C X (log X)^(-A) for every A > 0 and ε > 0 once H ≥ X^(2/15+ε), which improves on the 8/33 scale. Lean 4.30 kernel replays checked 100,596 declarations for the main theorem and 75,874 for Guth–Maynard, using only standard axioms. The main formal statement is AllCenterMAP.map_two_fifteenths.
