Convergence of a Parametric Symmetric Galerkin Boundary Element Method for the Mullins-Sekerka Flow

Tokuhiro Eto

TL;DR
The main convergence theorem and principal auxiliary results are formalized in Lean 4 with Mathlib, archived on Zenodo, and built on a prior Lean formalization.
Abstract
We prove convergence of the fully discrete parametric scheme for the two-phase Mullins-Sekerka flow of a closed curve in the plane. The scheme combines piecewise linear parametric curves, a curvature discretization of Barrett–Garcke–Nürnberg type and a symmetric Galerkin boundary element approximation of the Dirichlet-to-Neumann map. For a smooth solution whose curves satisfy a capacity condition, a uniform parameter mesh of size $h$, a constant time step $τ$ and exact boundary element integrals, we show that the discrete curves stay regular and embedded, and that their distance to the exact curves in the topology $W^{1,\infty}$, after reparametrization by arclength, tends to zero in the first order of the time-step size $τ$ whenever the mesh size $h$ is bounded by $c_0τ^2$ for some $c_0$.
Problem

Convergence of fully discrete parametric schemes for the two-phase Mullins-Sekerka flow of closed planar curves had not been proved. The scheme in question combines BGN-type curvature discretization with a symmetric Galerkin boundary element approximation of the Dirichlet-to-Neumann map.

Approach

The analysis first proves uniform smoothness and a first-order rate for the time-semidiscrete scheme. It then compares the fully discrete polygons with the interpolated semidiscrete curves, splitting the error into consistency, linearized-step, and remainder terms. The linearized step is controlled in an adapted norm, following Rao's analysis for curve shortening. The main theorem and its auxiliary results are formalized in Lean 4 (v4.28.0-rc1) with Mathlib, on top of a separate formalization of boundary element bounds, relying on five stated external hypotheses.

Results

For smooth solutions satisfying a capacity condition, the discrete curves stay regular and embedded. Their W^{1,∞} distance to the exact curves, after reparametrization by arclength, is O(τ) when h ≤ c0 τ².
