The Cost of an Unknown Horizon for the Last Iterate of Clipped SGD under Heavy-Tailed Noise

Yuxing Peng, Zhiqing Tang, Weijia Jia

cs.LG Oct 24, 2026 · v1
formalization mathlib ml-theory probability

TL;DR
Machine-checked proofs in Lean 4 with Mathlib of the lower bounds (Theorems 5, 11(i)) and the known-horizon last-iterate bound for clipped SGD.
Abstract
Training is often stopped at a data-dependent time, as in early stopping, and the last iterate is kept. Guarantees valid at such times must hold at all times simultaneously. We determine the optimal time-uniform rate for the last iterate of clipped SGD on smooth convex objectives under heavy-tailed noise with a bounded central $p$th moment, $1<p\le2$. A schedule without horizon or confidence inputs attains $\tilde O(t^{-β_p})$ at all times, with $β_p=2(p-1)/(3p-1)$, also almost surely, and no rule that steps along the stochastic gradient at its current iterate attains $\tilde O(t^{-r})$ for any $r>β_p$, even with adaptive step sizes and thresholds. This rate lies between the optimal rate $O(T^{-α_p})$ for a known horizon $T$, $α_p=(p-1)/p$, and the previous time-uniform rate $\tilde O(t^{-2(p-1)/(3p)})$ (Chezhegov et al., ICLR 2026); under finite variance, the exponents are $1/2$, $2/5$, and $1/3$. Their conjecture that a better schedule reaches the optimal rate thus holds with a known horizon, as shown by Liu and by Jiang et al., but fails without one. Querying gradients at the running average of the iterates attains $\tilde O(t^{-α_p})$ at all times, whereas heavy-ball momentum with geometric forgetting and polynomially bounded steps cannot improve on $β_p$.
Problem

Training is often stopped at a data-dependent time and the last iterate is kept, so useful guarantees must hold at all times simultaneously. The question is the optimal time-uniform high-probability rate for the last iterate of clipped SGD on smooth convex objectives under heavy-tailed noise with a bounded central pth moment, 1<p≤2.

Approach

A horizon-free and confidence-free schedule of polynomially decaying step sizes and growing clipping thresholds is analyzed with a recursively averaged suffix potential. A matching lower bound covers all rules that step along the stochastic gradient at the current iterate, including adaptive ones. Iterate averaging and clipped heavy-ball momentum are also analyzed. The lower bounds (Theorems 5 and 11(i)) and the known-horizon bound (Theorem 1) are machine-checked in Lean 4 with Mathlib.

Results

The time-uniform exponent β_p=2(p-1)/(3p-1) is optimal for current-iterate rules: 2/5 at p=2, compared with 1/2 for a known horizon and the previous 1/3. Querying gradients at the running average attains the known-horizon exponent α_p=(p-1)/p at all times. Heavy-ball momentum with geometric forgetting cannot beat β_p. Simulations show fitted slopes of about -0.5 for the known-horizon and averaging methods and about -0.3 to -0.35 for clipped SGD and heavy-ball momentum.
