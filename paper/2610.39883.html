Counterexamples to the pure semisimplicity conjecture and Coxeter type $\mathbb{I}_2(m)$

Junpeng Ren

TL;DR
The main theorem, counterexamples to the pure semisimplicity conjecture, is fully formalized in Lean 4 with Mathlib, with public code checked by kernel replay.
Abstract
Quantum chains of division rings yield counterexamples to the pure semisimplicity conjecture and hereditary artinian rings of every Coxeter type $\mathbb{I}_2(m)$ with $m \geq 4$. For a field element $q$ of multiplicative order $\ell \geq 2$, possibly infinite, the construction gives an extension $H \subseteq T$ with right dimension $2$ and left dimension $\ell$. If $\ell = \infty$, the associated triangular ring is right pure semisimple but neither left pure semisimple nor of finite representation type. Over $\mathbb{Q}$, the parameters $q = 2, 3, 4, \dots$ give pairwise Morita inequivalent counterexamples. If $\ell<\infty$, the ring is of finite representation type with Coxeter type $\mathbb{I}_2(\ell+2)$, and the right dimension sequence of ${}_HT_T$ is periodic with period $(1, 2, \ldots, 2, 1, \ell)$, where $2$ occurs $\ell-1$ times. Every finite $\ell \geq 2$ is realized by choosing $q$ to be a primitive $\ell$-th root of unity.
Problem

The pure semisimplicity conjecture asserts that every right pure semisimple ring is of finite representation type, which is equivalent to pure semisimplicity being left-right symmetric. Simson reduced the existence of a counterexample to a generalized Artin problem about dimension sequences of iterated dual bimodules of division ring extensions.

Approach

The author builds quantum chains of division rings from a field element q of multiplicative order ℓ, giving an extension H ⊆ T of right dimension 2 and left dimension ℓ. Embeddings of T into itself by conjugation by ordered products yield division subrings H_r satisfying flag conditions (B) and (F). These conditions determine the right dimension sequence. Simson's reflection-functor criterion then gives right pure semisimplicity of the triangular ring. The main theorem is formalized in Lean 4 with Mathlib.

Results

For ℓ = ∞, the triangular ring is right pure semisimple but neither left pure semisimple nor of finite representation type. Over ℚ, different q give pairwise Morita inequivalent counterexamples. For finite ℓ, the rings have finite representation type with Coxeter type I_2(ℓ+2), and the right dimension sequences are periodic. The Lean formalization passed a fresh kernel replay.
