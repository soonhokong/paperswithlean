The Hlawka-Schatten inequality: an exposition of recent progress

Seokbin Lee

math.FA Nov 23, 2026 · v1

Read PDF arXiv abstract
TL;DR
Exposits AI-produced Lean 4 formalizations of Hlawka-Schatten results, deriving its proof presentation from the Lean code and Prove2Me missions.
Abstract
We give an expository account of recent progress on a question of Audenaert and Kittaneh concerning a Hlawka-type inequality for Schatten norms of matrices. We review the classical Hornich-Hlawka inequality and explain the motivation behind understanding the so-called Hlawka-Schatten inequality. We present an elementary $2 \times 2$ construction showing that no finite constant satisfies the Hlawka-Schatten inequality for the trace norm, and explain Esa's proof that a dimension-independent constant exists for every $1 < p < \infty$. We also discuss the Hlawka-Schatten inequality for diagonal matrices and subsequent developments thereof on Prove2Me. Our aim is to make the mathematical ideas behind these AI-assisted results accessible, and to clarify their relationship with the available Lean formalizations.
Problem

Audenaert and Kittaneh asked whether a Hlawka-type inequality with some constant holds for Schatten p-norms of matrices. Recent AI-assisted answers to this question exist mainly as Lean formalizations, which makes the underlying mathematics hard to access.

Approach

The paper reviews the classical Hornich-Hlawka inequality and Hornich-Hlawka spaces. It then presents human-readable proofs extracted from the Lean 4 code of Esa's formalization, with an AI model used to help parse that code. It also covers the diagonal-matrix variant and related open missions on Prove2Me, a platform for autonomous formalization into Lean 4.

Results

An elementary 2x2 construction shows that no finite constant works for the trace norm (p=1). It also explains Esa's Lean-verified proof that a dimension-independent constant exists for every 1<p<∞. Open questions on l_p Hornich-Hlawka spaces and diagonal Schatten constants are summarized.
