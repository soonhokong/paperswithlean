Friedman–Stanley cofinality without Borel completeness

Ruijun Wang

TL;DR
The structural counterexample and its main supporting results (coset cofinality, bounded-tree Borelness, graph non-reduction) are formalized in Lean 4 with Mathlib.
Abstract
Friedman and Stanley asked whether an invariant Borel class of countable structures must be Borel complete whenever every countable level of their hierarchy reduces to it. We construct a counterexample in one fixed countable language. The structural counterexample and its principal supporting results have been formalized in Lean 4 and it is available on github.
Problem

Friedman and Stanley asked whether an invariant Borel class of countable structures must be Borel complete whenever every countable level of their tree-isomorphism hierarchy Borel-reduces to it.

Approach

The authors build a single invariant Borel class in one fixed countable language, parameterized by closed subgroups of S_∞ × S_∞, using ultrahomogeneous structures whose automorphism groups are the given subgroups. They show that coset relations of Polishable subgroups are cofinal in the Friedman–Stanley hierarchy, using a layered coding with finite contexts and Boolean matrices, and then show that graph isomorphism does not reduce to the class. The structural theorem and four supporting statements are formalized in Lean 4.30.0 against a pinned Mathlib revision.

Results

The paper gives a negative answer to the Friedman–Stanley question: there is an L_{ω1ω} sentence to which every countable Friedman–Stanley level reduces, while graph isomorphism does not reduce to it. The Lean proof uses only standard axioms and contains no unfinished placeholders. The López–Escobar step and the Appendix A arguments are not formalized.
