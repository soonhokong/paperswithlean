Problem

The entry studies arithmetic regular configurations built with private prime pivots. It asks how Palm mass identities and deficit bounds behave, and whether cumulant activity obstructs regularity.

Approach

Exact source–target Palm mass identities are formalized in Lean. The formalization also covers ordinary deletion arguments and normalized deficit bounds for the configurations. Regularity of an independent target and lower bounds on absolute cumulant activity are proved at prime scales.

Results

The independent target is regular with probability tending to one. Absolute cumulant activity has a positive normalized lower bound on prime scales, for both the original and stronger retained sets. The registry machine-checked the proofs.
