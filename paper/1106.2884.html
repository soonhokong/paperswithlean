On $δ$-superderivations of simple superalgebras of Jordan brackets

Ivan Kaygorodov, Victor Zhelyabin

math.RA Jun 15, 2011 · v2 math.QA
formalization algebra
Read PDF arXiv abstract
TL;DR
Corrected statements, including the characteristic-3 remarks and the relation 3μD(a)=0, are reported as verified in Lean.
Abstract
A complete description of $δ$-derivations and $δ$-superderivations is given for simple unital superalgebras of Jordan brackets over a field of characteristic different from $2$ (and, for the odd $δ$-superderivations, different from $3$; see Remark A for characteristic $3$) and for simple unital finite-dimensional Jordan superalgebras over an algebraically closed field of characteristic $p\neq2$ (with the corrections of Remarks A and C for $p>2$). As a consequence, a criterion for simple unital superalgebras of Jordan brackets to be special is obtained.
Problem

The work classifies δ-derivations and δ-superderivations of simple unital superalgebras of Jordan brackets, and of simple unital finite-dimensional Jordan superalgebras in characteristic p≠2. The published version contained statements that fail in characteristic 3.

Approach

Kantor-double superalgebras J(Γ,{,}) are analysed through structural lemmas on the bracket, such as Γ=Γ{Γ,Γ}. A 1/2-derivation is characterised as multiplication by an element of Γ_0 satisfying a bracket compatibility condition. The analysis is extended to finite-dimensional Jordan superalgebras, including the characteristic-3 alternative superalgebras B(1,2) and B(4,2). The corrected statements and characteristic-3 remarks are reported as checked in Lean.

Results

A complete description of δ-derivations and δ-superderivations is given, together with a criterion for simple unital superalgebras of Jordan brackets to be special. Remark 4, Corollary 5 and Theorems 10 and 15 are corrected for p=3, where nontrivial odd 1/2-superderivations exist.
