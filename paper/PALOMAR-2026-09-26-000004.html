Abstract
This project formalizes the existence of a model companion for the first-order theory of groups satisfying x^3 = 1. Its principal group-theoretic result gives a uniform, explicit bound on the number of generators needed to witness a failure of amalgamation with an existentially closed exponent-three group. The development follows the accompanying manuscript through free groups, associated graded Lie algebras, strict extensions, and the bounded-amalgamation criterion.

Problem

The goal is to establish that the first-order theory of groups satisfying x^3 = 1 has a model companion. The key group-theoretic difficulty is bounding the number of generators needed to witness a failure of amalgamation with an existentially closed exponent-three group.

Approach

The development follows an accompanying manuscript and formalizes it in Lean. It builds up free groups, associated graded Lie algebras, and strict extensions. These are then used to verify a bounded-amalgamation criterion that implies existence of the model companion.

Results

A machine-checked Lean proof, verified by the Palomar registry, that exponent-3 groups have a model companion. The proof includes a uniform, explicit generator bound for witnessing amalgamation failures.
