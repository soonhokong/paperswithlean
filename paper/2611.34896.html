Weakly Pointed Fusion $Π$-supercategories over fields of characteristic not two

Kotaro Kawasumi

math.QA Nov 25, 2026 · v1
formalization category-theory algebra
Read PDF arXiv abstract Code
TL;DR
The real-field case, including an explicit construction of a Brauer–Wall reference category over R, is formalized in Lean 4 with a public artifact.
Abstract
We study a class of weakly pointed fusion $Π$-supercategories over a field $k$ of characteristic not equal to $2$, with unit $\mathbf 1$ satisfying $\mathrm{End}(\mathbf 1) = k$. Assuming the existence of a Brauer–Wall reference category over $k$, we classify these categories up to monoidal equivalence. We show that Brauer–Wall reference categories exist for $k = \mathbb C, \mathbb R$ but not for $k = \mathbb F_5$. For each finite group $G$, we also study a product of these categories equipped with a $G$-grading and define a group $\mathcal T_k(G)$, giving an explicit formula for the multiplication in terms of group cochains for $k = \mathbb C$ and $\mathbb R$.
Problem

The paper classifies weakly pointed fusion Π-supercategories with split unit over fields of characteristic not two, up to monoidal equivalence. The motivation comes from fermionic anomalies of finite group symmetries in 1+1 dimensions.

Approach

Each category determines a homomorphism to the Brauer–Wall group BW(k). Assuming a Brauer–Wall reference category exists, the categories are classified by twists, up to gauge equivalence. Existence of reference categories is controlled by an obstruction o_4(k), defined using an extension-theory argument in the style of ENO. A stacking product of G-graded categories defines a group T_k(G), and the real case was formalized in Lean 4 [Kaw26].

Results

Reference categories exist for k = C and R but not for k = F_5. Explicit cochain formulas are given for the stacking multiplication over C and R, along with an exact sequence 0 → SH_k(G) → T_k(G) → Z^1(G, BW(k)) → 0. The R case, including an explicit reference category, was machine-checked in Lean 4.31.0.
