Problem

Chvátal's conjecture states that a hereditary family of sets has a star, the subfamily containing a fixed element, of maximum size among its intersecting subfamilies. Chang, Liu, and Liu gave a proof using a sharp correlation inequality, and this entry machine-checks that argument.

Approach

The paper's arguments are formalized in Lean 4 using Mathlib. The formalization covers the star theorem for hereditary set families and the sharp Boolean Fourier correlation inequality. It also covers the antipodal corollary, the sharpness results, and a weighted strengthening. According to the entry, the formalization was prepared entirely by GPT-6.
