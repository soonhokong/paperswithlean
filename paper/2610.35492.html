Uniform large-genus asymptotics of Weil-Petersson volumes

Qihang Wang, Zhiyi Li, Bolin Qiu, Shuaijia Wang, Yuan Zhong, Kun Chen, Renqiu Xia

TL;DR
Results on Weil–Petersson volume asymptotics are accompanied by a Lean 4 formalization in the WPFormalization repository.
Abstract
We prove an expansion in $1/g$ of the Weil–Petersson volumes $V_{g,n}$ that is uniform over all cusp counts, from fixed $n$ to arbitrarily large $n/g$. An explicit leading term and single-factorial remainder bounds give exponentially small relative errors after truncation at a suitable order proportional to $g$. A positive recursion along the string flow connects these volumes to pure $ψ$-class intersection numbers, where Painlevé I fixes the normalization; uniform coefficient extraction then recovers individual volumes. We determine Zograf's constant $C=1/\sqrtπ$ and resolve Mirzakhani's two questions on volume ratios for growing cusp counts and adjacent coefficient ratios uniformly over all degrees. Via Mirzakhani's integration formula, the same volume estimates control random surfaces at two distinct scales. At $n/\sqrt g\to c\in(0,\infty)$, the entire positive Laplace spectrum below $1/4$ converges to an explicit Poisson point process generated by geodesics cutting off two cusps. Together with Huang's theorem, this characterizes the transition for $n\ge1$: the spectral gap tends in probability to $1/4$ exactly when $n/\sqrt g\to0$, and to $0$ exactly when $n/\sqrt g\to\infty$. At density $n/g\toλ\in(0,\infty)$, we identify the local limit around a typical point; its cusp-filling is conformally a disc. As $λ\to\infty$, these limits approach the infinite Weil–Petersson sphere of Budd and Curien. We prove their parabolicity conjecture and, by a growing-radius coupling, answer their scaling-limit question: after deep cusps are trimmed, the rescaled infinite sphere converges to the Brownian plane.
Problem

Known asymptotics of Weil–Petersson volumes V_{g,n} cover fixed n as g grows or fixed g as n grows, but give no uniform approximation when both parameters grow. Related open questions include Zograf's constant, Mirzakhani's volume-ratio questions, and the spectral behavior of random surfaces with many cusps.

Approach

The Weil–Petersson intersection brackets are deformed into pure psi-class intersection numbers, yielding a positive recursion along the string flow. This recursion is compared, uniformly in the degrees and the deformation parameter, with an explicit solvable harmonic model, using random-walk arguments. Painlevé I fixes the normalization, and uniform coefficient extraction recovers individual volumes. An accompanying Lean 4 formalization is provided in the WPFormalization repository.

Results

The paper proves a 1/g expansion of V_{g,n} that is uniform in n, determines Zograf's constant C=1/sqrt(pi), and resolves Mirzakhani's two volume-ratio questions. It shows that the small Laplace spectrum converges to a Poisson process when n/sqrt(g)→c, characterizes the spectral-gap transition, and proves the Budd–Curien parabolicity conjecture together with convergence of the rescaled infinite Weil–Petersson sphere to the Brownian plane.
