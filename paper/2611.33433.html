Differentiability of the shape function in the planar corner growth model

Abstract
We prove that the shape function of planar directed last-passage percolation with general i.i.d.\ real-valued weights is differentiable on the open first quadrant, under a moment assumption of order $p''>4$ on the positive part of the weight and a nearly optimal order $p'>1$ on the negative part. Due to known results, only directions outside a percolation cone need be considered. The proof is a multiscale path-counting argument that uses the shape theorem, oriented percolation on a coarse-grained lattice, a simple change of measure in a finite region, and the van den Berg–Kesten inequality. Our Lean-formalized proof is an AI-assisted adaptation and improvement of the OpenAI proof of differentiability in planar first-passage percolation.
Problem

Whether the shape function of planar directed last-passage percolation (the corner growth model) with general i.i.d. real-valued weights is differentiable on the open first quadrant, under weak moment assumptions.

Approach

Only directions outside the percolation cone need treatment. The proof is a multiscale path-counting argument that combines the shape theorem, coarse-grained oriented percolation, a local change of measure defining a characteristic scale, and the van den Berg–Kesten inequality applied to disjoint witnesses. It adapts the OpenAI proof for first-passage percolation. The results were formalized in Lean 4 using a modified version of Scott Armstrong's Lean AutoFormalization skills, with LLM orchestrators.
