TL;DR

Abstract
A Lean 4 formalization, on top of Mathlib, of the classical function theory of several complex variables on open subsets of finite-dimensional complex normed spaces, with values in complex Banach spaces wherever the statements allow. The compared statements cover: Cauchy's integral formula on polydiscs, Osgood's lemma, the equivalence of holomorphy and analyticity, the Cauchy-Riemann equations, Cauchy estimates, the identity theorem, the maximum modulus principle, holomorphic dependence of integrals and the Cauchy-Pompeiu identity; the theorems of Weierstrass, Montel and Vitali and an estimate for holomorphic L^p classes; the holomorphic inverse and implicit mapping theorems; Reinhardt domains, logarithmic convexity, the characterization of convergence domains of power series, Laurent expansion, and continuation from Reinhardt and circular domains; Hartogs-Taylor expansion, Hartogs' continuity theorem, Hartogs' theorem on separate analyticity, removability of isolated singularities, the two Riemann extension theorems and Hartogs' extension theorem for compact holes; elementary analytic sets and their regular points; the local ring of analytic germs, Weierstrass division and preparation, and the Noetherian and unique factorization properties of the germ ring; injective holomorphic maps, Cartan's uniqueness theorem, linearity of biholomorphisms of circular domains, transitivity of the automorphisms of the ball and the inequivalence of ball and polydisc; holomorphic hulls, domains of holomorphy, Thullen's lemma, the Cartan-Thullen theorem and Bochner's tube theorem; subharmonic and plurisubharmonic functions, the Levi form, pseudoconvexity of domains of holomorphy, Levi's theorem and local peak functions; Runge domains and polynomial hulls. All results are classical; none is claimed to be new.
