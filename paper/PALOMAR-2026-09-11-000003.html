TL;DR
Formalizes in Lean 4 Younis's two-set lower-bound construction for sets avoiding {x, x+y, x+y²}, instantiated to prove exponent 0.77028.
Abstract
A Lean 4 proof of Younis's lower-bound construction for subsets of {1,...,N} with no configuration {x, x+y, x+y^2} (y nonzero), in its two-set form: for every square-free modulus m and residue sets R1, R2 modulo m with R1 square-difference-free and no nonzero difference of R2 equal to the square of a difference of R1, the interval {1,...,N} contains a configuration-free set of size at least C(rho) N^rho for every rho below 1/2 + log|R1|/(3 log m) + log|R2|/(6 log m). The theorem is instantiated at m = 145 with |R1| = 10 and |R2| = 32, giving the exponent 0.770287..., which is proved to exceed Younis's exponent 0.768503... (m = 65, sizes 7 and 17) by exact comparisons of integer powers; decimal bounds 0.7702 < gamma < 0.77029 are also proved, and the bound is stated in liminf form for the counting function D(N).

Problem

The goal is a lower bound on the size of subsets of {1,...,N} with no configuration {x, x+y, x+y²} with y nonzero. Younis gave a construction from residue sets modulo a square-free modulus, reaching exponent 0.768503... with m = 65.

Approach

The two-set form of Younis's construction is proved in Lean 4. For a square-free modulus m and residue sets R1, R2 satisfying square-difference conditions, {1,...,N} contains a configuration-free set of size at least C(rho) N^rho for every rho below 1/2 + log|R1|/(3 log m) + log|R2|/(6 log m). The theorem is then instantiated at m = 145 with |R1| = 10 and |R2| = 32.

Results

The instantiation gives exponent 0.770287..., proved to exceed Younis's 0.768503... by exact comparisons of integer powers. Decimal bounds 0.7702 < gamma < 0.77029 are also proved, and the bound is stated in liminf form for the counting function D(N).
