Median eigenvalues of subcubic graphs

Hricha Acharya, Benjamin Jeter, Zilin Jiang

TL;DR
The case analysis behind key lemmas is formalized in Lean 4 with AI assistance (Codex/GPT), and the Lean project is shipped as arXiv ancillary files.
Abstract
We show that the median eigenvalues of every connected graph of maximum degree at most three, except for the Heawood graph, are at most $1$ in absolute value, resolving open problems posed by Fowler and Pisanski, and by Mohar.
Problem

The question is whether the median eigenvalues of every connected subcubic graph lie in [-1,1], the Heawood graph excepted. This resolves open problems of Fowler–Pisanski and Mohar.

Approach

The argument uses Cauchy interlacing through positive and negative tail estimates relative to a maximum cut. It then performs an extensive case analysis of local configurations: cut enhancers and positive and negative tail reducers. As an independent check, the case analysis and its use in Lemmas 2.7 and 2.8 were formalized in Lean 4 with Codex and an OpenAI GPT model. The authors reviewed the formal statements.

Results

The median eigenvalues of every connected subcubic graph other than the Heawood graph are at most 1 in absolute value, and a positive-fraction strengthening is also proved. The ancillary Lean project builds successfully.
