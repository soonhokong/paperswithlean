A Counterexample to the Trace Problem for Uniform Tracial Completions

Sauers

Palomar math.OA Sep 14, 2026 · v1

TL;DR
Formalizes in Lean, with machine-checked unconditional theorems, a counterexample to the trace problem for uniform tracial completions of C*-algebras.
Abstract
We formalize a negative answer to Problem XXII of Schafhauser, Tikuisis and White. There is a separable unital C*-algebra A with nonempty compact tracial state space such that its uniform tracial completion M has a tracial state that is discontinuous in the uniform 2-norm. Thus the canonical extensions of the traces of A form a proper subset X of the traces of M. A second theorem gives a factorial tracially complete pair (M, X) with the same failure, answering Question 1.1 of Carrion, Castillejos, Evington, Gabe, Schafhauser, Tikuisis and White negatively. In both statements, X is exactly the set of uniform-2-norm-continuous traces. The proof uses a unitized c0-sum of antipodally covariant matrix-function algebras of growing rank and dimension. Borsuk-Ulam supplies finite coordinate-state constraints; weak-star compactness produces a corona trace detecting a sequence whose uniform 2-norm tends to zero. Both selected Lean theorems are unconditional. The completion is characterized by tracial completeness, unique restriction of designated traces to all base traces, and density in the uniform 2-norm.

Problem

Problem XXII of Schafhauser, Tikuisis and White asks whether every trace on the uniform tracial completion of a C*-algebra comes from traces of the original algebra. A related question of Carrion et al. asks the same for factorial tracially complete pairs.

Approach

The counterexample algebra is a unitized c0-sum of antipodally covariant matrix-function algebras of growing rank and dimension. Borsuk-Ulam supplies finite coordinate-state constraints. Weak-star compactness then produces a corona trace that detects a sequence whose uniform 2-norm tends to zero. The completion is characterized in Lean by tracial completeness, unique restriction of designated traces to all base traces, and density in the uniform 2-norm.

Results

A separable unital C*-algebra is constructed whose uniform tracial completion has a tracial state that is discontinuous in the uniform 2-norm, answering Problem XXII negatively. A factorial tracially complete pair with the same failure answers Question 1.1 of Carrion et al. negatively. Both selected Lean theorems are unconditional.
