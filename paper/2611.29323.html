Sharp Volume Contraction of a Centrally Cut John Ellipsoid

Zhou Longfei

TL;DR

Abstract
For a convex body $K\subset\mathbb R^n$ in positive dimension, let $w(K)$ be the volume of its maximum-volume inscribed ellipsoid. For a closed halfspace $H$ whose boundary passes through the center of that ellipsoid, we prove \[ w(K\cap H)\le R_nw(K),\qquad R_1=\frac12,\qquad R_n=\frac12\left(\frac n{n-1}\right)^{(n-1)/2}\quad(n\ge2), \] with equality attained in every fixed dimension. This refines the optimal dimension-independent bound from the author's earlier work on [Khachiyan's ellipsoid conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28447). We characterize the full equality family, including nonsimplex extremizers, and establish an exact volume-deficit identity that yields dimension-independent stability of the new ellipsoid and its weighted contact geometry in John coordinates. The proof reduces the problem to finite John contacts and constructs an explicit conic certificate by a transformation of contact directions and a common polar rotation. Its determinant is controlled by a nonnegative contact defect. The sharp contraction theorem, equality classification, stability estimates, and supporting results have been formally verified in Lean 4 using mathlib.
Problem

The question is how much the volume of the maximum-volume inscribed (John) ellipsoid of a convex body can shrink when the body is cut by a halfspace through the ellipsoid's center. A sharp dimension-dependent bound and characterization of extremizers were open, refining an earlier dimension-independent bound related to Khachiyan's ellipsoid conjecture.

Approach

The problem is reduced to finite John contact polytopes. An explicit conic (second-order-cone dual) certificate is then built by transforming contact directions and applying a common polar rotation. Its determinant is controlled by a nonnegative contact defect. An exact volume-deficit identity splits the losses into cone-pairing slack and a matrix divergence term, and is used for equality and stability analysis.

Results

The bound w(K∩H) ≤ R_n w(K), with R_1=1/2 and R_n=(1/2)(n/(n-1))^{(n-1)/2}, is proved sharp and attained in every dimension. The full equality family, including nonsimplex extremizers, is characterized, and dimension-independent stability estimates are given.
