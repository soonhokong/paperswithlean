Problem

Hyperbolicity results for Jensen polynomials of Riemann's xi-function depend on precise asymptotics of the centered xi coefficients. These analytic inputs need a rigorous, machine-checked treatment.

Approach

The development formalizes the exact normalization of the centered-xi coefficients. It proves a uniform sectorial saddle-point asymptotic for them. It also establishes relative-error derivative estimates up to order six. The proofs were machine-checked by the Palomar registry.

Results

The formalized statements are the analytic inputs used by a two-thirds hyperbolicity-wedge theorem for Jensen polynomials of the xi-function. The final Jensen-polynomial hyperbolicity theorem itself is not stated in this topic.
