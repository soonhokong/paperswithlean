Rational invariants of three frames in the split Albert algebra: a Lean formalization

Robert R. MacGregor

Palomar math.RA Sep 13, 2026 · v1
formalization algebra
View on Palomar Code
TL;DR
A Lean 4 development proving that the 54 trace invariants of three frames in the split Albert algebra generate a field of index two in the full field of rational invariants.
Abstract
Let J be the split Albert algebra over a field k of characteristic zero, the 27-dimensional Jordan algebra of 3 by 3 Hermitian matrices over the split octonions, with trace tr. A frame is an ordered triple of trace-one idempotents summing to 1. Three frames E, F, K carry 54 natural invariants under the group G of all unital k-linear bijections of J preserving its Jordan product: the pair traces tr(eᵢ∘fⱼ), tr(fⱼ∘kₗ), tr(kₗ∘eᵢ) and the triple traces tr(eᵢ∘(fⱼ∘kₗ)). This Lean 4 development proves that these traces do not generate all rational invariants of three frames, and that adjoining one additional invariant suffices: the field of G-invariant rational functions on the variety of frame triples is a quadratic extension H(d) of the field H generated by the 54 traces, where d = tr(W∘(W∘W)), W = x∘(y∘z) − y∘(x∘z), x = e₂ − e₀, y = f₂ − f₀, z = k₂ − k₀. The development also proves that the variety of frame triples is irreducible, that the coordinate algebra used is the standard split Albert algebra, that d is not in H, and that every invariant is uniquely a + b·d with a, b in H. The ambiguity occurs over ℚ: two explicit frame triples have identical 54 traces and d = −2591357/62208 and −1782559/62208, and no ℚ-linear automorphism of J combined with any of the 1,296 relabellings of frames and idempotents carries one to the other. The proved endpoints use only the permitted axiom dependencies propext, Quot.sound and Classical.choice; a Python script rechecks the example in exact arithmetic.

Problem

Three frames (ordered triples of trace-one idempotents summing to 1) in the split Albert algebra J carry 54 natural pair and triple trace invariants under the automorphism group of J. The question is whether these traces generate the whole field of G-invariant rational functions on the variety of frame triples.

Approach

In Lean 4, the variety of frame triples is shown to be irreducible, and the coordinate algebra is identified with the standard split Albert algebra. An extra invariant d = tr(W∘(W∘W)) is introduced, with W built from Jordan commutator-type expressions in differences of idempotents. The development proves that d lies outside the trace field H and that every invariant can be written uniquely as a + b·d with a, b in H. A Python script separately rechecks a rational counterexample in exact arithmetic.

Results

The field of rational invariants is the quadratic extension H(d) of H, so the 54 traces alone do not suffice. Over ℚ, two frame triples share all 54 traces but have different values of d, and no automorphism combined with any of the 1,296 relabellings relates them. The proofs depend only on propext, Quot.sound and Classical.choice.
