Abstract
This paper gives a new path to determine all $\mathbb{F}_q$-linear relations among multiple zeta values in positive characteristic, which had been done by Huang, Lai, Li and the author. The path used in this paper is based on the idea of operator generation, which originated from the work of Todd, developed by the author, and independently by Im-Kim-Ngo Dac.
Problem

The goal is to determine all F_q-linear relations among Thakur's multiple zeta values in positive characteristic. This was previously done by Huang, Lai, Li and the author; the paper gives a new route to the same result.

Approach

The method uses operator generation, building on work of Todd, the author, and Im-Kim-Ngo Dac. It constructs binary relations and operators on Carlitz multiple polylogarithm relations, then proves that the BCP relations are complete. It also computes the dimension of the relation space via relations among subspaces. A second completeness proof uses minimal-term elimination in the (depth, lex) order.

Results

The BCP relations are shown to generate all F_q-linear relations, and the dimensions of the relation spaces are determined. A Lean 4 formalization of the proofs is linked.
