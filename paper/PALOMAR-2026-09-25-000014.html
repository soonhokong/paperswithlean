Problem

Sunflower bounds limit how large a family of fixed-size sets can be without containing a sunflower. Several modern bounds improve on the classical Erdős–Rado theorem, and the entry sets out to formalize them in Lean.

Approach

The Lean development proves the classical Erdős–Rado theorem, the Alweiss–Lovett–Wu–Zhang (ALWZ) bound, and the Rao and Bell–Chueluecha–Warnke refinements. It also covers upper and lower bounds for robust sunflowers and the BCW spread and satisfying results. Ten explicit finite statements are compared, including second-moment and Janson-inequality routes to the ALWZ bound.

Results

The registry machine-checked the proofs for all listed sunflower and robust-sunflower bounds, which include alternative proof routes to the ALWZ bound.
