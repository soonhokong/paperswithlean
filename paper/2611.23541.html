Depth Hierarchies for Symmetric Algebraic Circuits

Anuj Dawar, Prateek Dwivedi, Benedikt Pago, Tim Seppelt

cs.CC Nov 17, 2026 · v1
formalization logic
Read PDF arXiv abstract
TL;DR
Main results, including the depth hierarchy theorems, definitions of symmetric circuits/ABPs and symVNP, are formalised in Lean with an archived artifact.
Abstract
Algebraic complexity studies the cost of computing families of polynomials by algebraic circuits, with the central goal of proving super-polynomial circuit lower bounds and separations between different models of algebraic computation. Since such lower bounds remain elusive for general circuits, a common strategy is to focus on restricted models. A recent line of work (ICALP 2020, ITCS & STOC 2026) introduces and studies symmetry as a syntactic circuit restriction, showing exponential symmetric lower bounds for the permanent, as well as separations between symmetric analogues of algebraic formulas ($\mathsf{VF}$), skew circuits ($\mathsf{VS}$), and circuits ($\mathsf{VP}$). We investigate the role of depth as a complexity parameter in symmetric circuits. For every fixed $Δ\geq 1$, we exhibit a family of polynomials computable by symmetric circuits of polynomial size and depth $2Δ+3$, but requiring size $n^{Ω(\log n)}$ at depth $Δ$. We also define symmetric versions of the bounded-depth algebraic complexity classes $\mathsf{VAC}^k$, for all $k \in \mathbb{N}$, that we call $\mathsf{symVAC}^k$, and establish two strict hierarchies: Firstly, a hierarchy within $\mathsf{symVAC}^0$, and secondly, a hierarchy of the form $\mathsf{symVAC}^k \subsetneq \mathsf{symVAC}^{k+1}$ for all $k$. The latter shows that symmetric circuits of depth $(\log n)^{k+1}$ are strictly more powerful than depth-$(\log n)^k$ circuits. Moreover, we place the bounded-depth classes within the landscape of symmetric algebraic complexity developed by Dwivedi, Pago and Seppelt (STOC 2026). We complement this picture by introducing $\mathsf{symVNP}$, a symmetric analogue of $\mathsf{VNP}$, and establishing $\mathsf{symVNP}$-completeness of the permanent under symmetric projections.
Problem

Super-polynomial lower bounds for general algebraic circuits are out of reach, so restricted models such as symmetric circuits are studied. The role of depth as a complexity parameter in symmetric algebraic circuits had not been characterised.

Approach

Matrix-symmetric circuits are translated into linear combinations of homomorphism polynomials, with support size and depth corresponding to the width and depth of tree decompositions (Δ-forestwidth). Separation arguments show that homomorphism polynomials of full n-ary trees cannot be expressed using patterns of small forestwidth. Symmetric bounded-depth classes symVAC^k, symmetric ABPs, and symVNP are defined. The main results were formalised in Lean with AI assistance, and the formalisation is available on an archive.

Results

For each fixed Δ, there are polynomials computable by polynomial-size symmetric circuits of depth 2Δ+3 that require size n^{Ω(log n)} at depth Δ. Both the symVAC^0 hierarchy and the symVAC^k hierarchy are strict. The paper also shows symVAC^0 = symVF, symVP = symVAC^1 on p-families, symVBP = symVS, and that the permanent is symVNP-complete under symmetric projections.
