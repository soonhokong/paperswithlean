Hausdorff dimension of planar box-like self-affine sets with rotations

Antti Käenmäki, R. Dániel Prokaj, Károly Simon, Sascha Troscheit

TL;DR
The paper's main dimension results and their proofs are formalized in Lean 4 over Mathlib, with AI-model assistance and a public GitHub repository.
Abstract
We study the Hausdorff dimension of proximal planar self-affine sets whose defining maps have diagonal or anti-diagonal linear parts, with both types present. Such systems are irreducible but not strongly irreducible. Under a projective exponential separation condition, we show that the Hausdorff dimension equals the minimum of $2$ and the affinity dimension, thereby extending the dimension formula of Hochman–Rapaport to all proximal irreducible planar systems. When the affinity dimension is at most an explicit threshold determined by the Lyapunov exponents, the formula holds assuming only that there are no exact overlaps. Above the threshold, it holds under the strong open set condition. These dimension formulae are proved by approximation with finite diagonal subsystems. Assuming projective exponential separation, we also determine the pointwise dimensions of the coordinate projections of the equilibrium state. Below the threshold, the projections are not exact-dimensional: the pointwise dimension of each exists almost everywhere and takes exactly two values, each on a set of positive measure. At or above the threshold, both projections are exact-dimensional with dimension one.
Problem

The problem is to determine the Hausdorff dimension of proximal planar self-affine sets whose maps have diagonal or anti-diagonal linear parts, with both types present. Such systems are irreducible but not strongly irreducible, so they fall outside earlier results of Hochman–Rapaport and Rapaport.

Approach

Under a projective exponential separation condition, the dimension formula is obtained by approximating the system with finite diagonal subsystems. A threshold determined by the Lyapunov exponents separates two cases: below it only the absence of exact overlaps is assumed, and above it the strong open set condition is assumed. The pointwise dimensions of the coordinate projections of the equilibrium state are also analysed. The results are formalized in Lean 4 over Mathlib using only Lean's standard axioms and no unproved literature hypotheses.

Results

The Hausdorff dimension equals min{2, affinity dimension}, which extends the Hochman–Rapaport formula to all proximal irreducible planar systems. Below the threshold, the coordinate projections are double-dimensional, with exactly two pointwise dimension values, each taken on a set of positive measure. At or above the threshold, both projections are exact-dimensional with dimension one.
