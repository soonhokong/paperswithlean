Problem

The integrality gap of the subtour LP relaxation for metric TSP is known to be below 3/2, following the Karlin–Klein–Oveis Gharan (KKO) work. The task is to give machine-checked proofs of an explicit bound of this kind.

Approach

The Lean development proves that every metric TSP instance on at least three vertices has a Hamiltonian tour whose cost is bounded relative to any feasible subtour-LP objective. It exposes the exact finite-layer saving. It also formalizes a bound within the KKO framework using GKL capacity estimates.

Results

Every metric instance on at least three vertices has a tour of cost at most (3/2 - 2.05522e-30) times any feasible subtour-LP value. A strictly larger saving holds uniformly over all instances.
