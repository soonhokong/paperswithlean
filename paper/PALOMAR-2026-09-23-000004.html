Abstract
Source-based formalization of the classical Stallings finite-graph recognition theorem. It gives an executable finite folding construction for every finite list of words in the rank-two free group, with exact subgroup-membership correctness. The least deterministic fold congruence is computed by finite exhaustive search; there is no complexity claim or implementation of core trimming, subgroup bases, intersections, or index algorithms.

Problem

Stallings' theorem says finitely generated subgroups of a free group are recognized by finite folded graphs. The goal is a verified, executable version of this recognition for the rank-two free group F₂.

Approach

The development gives an executable finite folding construction that takes any finite list of words in F₂. It computes the least deterministic fold congruence by finite exhaustive search. It proves that the resulting automaton decides membership in the generated subgroup exactly. The registry machine-checked the proofs.

Results

The formalization establishes exact subgroup-membership correctness for the folding automaton. It makes no complexity claim and does not implement core trimming, subgroup bases, intersections, or index algorithms.
