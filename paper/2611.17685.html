Semistable 1-Motives: Monodromy, Explicit Periods, and Local Duality

Mohammadreza Mohajer

TL;DR

Abstract
Let $K/\mathbf Q_p$ be unramified, with $p>2$. For a $1$-motive with constant lattice, split torus, and good abelian quotient, we compute the semistable extension complex from toric valuations and formal-group logarithms. A canonical monodromy boundary exists for every semistable representation; it detects splitting over crystalline periods and the increase in monodromy rank. For the stated $1$-motives, this boundary and the unramified quotient recover the restricted local Tate pairing, with exponential radicals. Nonsplit elliptic examples, Raynaud uniformization, and a height-one positive-period bound explain the geometric range. For toric motives we construct actual periods, convergent Robba bases, and integral action matrices. A toric basis family has perfect projective/trace-zero matrix duality, including its exact integral defect. An explicit residue-zero solution operator constructs every dual Herr class. Applied componentwise, the same operator gives relative full dual lifts for general constant-lattice $1$-motives once their coefficient sequence is specified. Finally, finite-base coefficient traces realize division-tower Iwasawa cohomology for any Tate lattice, and the period cup pairing equals the arithmetic group-ring pairing.
Problem

The paper develops explicit counterparts, for semistable 1-motives over unramified extensions of Q_p with p>2, of three classical constructions. These are the valuation and logarithm description of Kummer classes, local Tate duality with Bloch–Kato conditions, and period descriptions of Iwasawa cohomology.

Approach

The semistable extension complex is computed from toric valuations and formal-group logarithms. A canonical monodromy boundary is constructed for every semistable representation. For toric motives, the paper builds actual periods, convergent Robba bases, integral action matrices, and an explicit residue-zero solution operator for dual Herr classes. Selected algebraic results were formalized and checked in Lean 4.

Results

The monodromy boundary together with the unramified quotient recovers the restricted local Tate pairing. A toric basis family is shown to have perfect projective/trace-zero matrix duality. Finite-base coefficient traces realize division-tower Iwasawa cohomology, and the period cup pairing is shown to equal the arithmetic group-ring pairing.
