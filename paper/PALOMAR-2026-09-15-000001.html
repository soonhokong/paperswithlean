Boundary regularity for non-local operators with symmetric kernels and vanishing horizon

Philipp Svinger

TL;DR
Lean 4 formalization of the two main boundary Hölder regularity theorems for nonlocal operators with symmetric kernels and vanishing horizon.
Abstract
This Lean 4 formalization proves the two main theorems of the paper 'Boundary regularity for non-local operators with symmetric kernels and vanishing horizon' (arXiv:2504.00567v1). For a fractional order s in (1/2,1), the paper studies a nonlocal operator with symmetric kernel whose interaction horizon depends on the distance to the boundary and on a horizon parameter sigma in (0,1], and shrinks near the boundary. The formalization proves that weak solutions u to the Dirichlet problem with zero Dirichlet data and bounded right-hand side are in the Hölder space C^{2s-1} up to the boundary. Furthermore, it is proven that the quotient u/d_Omega^{2s-1} is also Hölder continuous up to the boundary. Both results include norm estimates controlled by the essential supremum of the right-hand side.
