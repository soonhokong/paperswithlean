Problem

The work establishes Poisson limit laws for a run field that records positions, all excesses and signs. It treats these laws under conditioning on small primes, and requires the proofs to be machine-checked.

Approach

Hard and soft small-prime conditioning are formalized in Lean, together with deterministic masks and statistics and stable recorded variables. An information-adapted cutoff is constructed that respects a prescribed conditioning floor.

Results

The formalization includes Poisson laws for the run field, uniform quenched convergence, and the information-adapted cutoff. The Palomar registry machine-checked the proofs.
