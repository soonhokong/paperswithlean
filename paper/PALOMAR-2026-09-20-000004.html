Problem

Establish Poisson comparisons for occurrences of words from dictionaries. The comparisons cover uniformly sampled fixed-size and affine dictionaries, with each dictionary held fixed inside the comparison.

Approach

The results are formalized in Lean and the proofs are machine-checked by the Palomar registry. The formalization covers mean distances for typical dictionaries and keeps exact excess terms and their signs. It also includes aggregation over categories and geometric marks.
