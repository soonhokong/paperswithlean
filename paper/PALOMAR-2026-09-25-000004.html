Problem

The classical Seifert–van Kampen theorem for fundamental groupoids states that for an open cover X = U ∪ V, the square of fundamental groupoids of U ∩ V, U, V, and X is a pushout. The goal is to state and prove this for Mathlib's ordinary continuous-path fundamental groupoid without basepoint, connectedness, or separation hypotheses.

Approach

The proof works with continuous paths and endpoint-preserving homotopies and keeps all points as objects. An auxiliary preorder is specialized to the indiscrete relation, and a natural equivalence with Mathlib's ordinary fundamental groupoid is established. The categorical descent functor is built by path subdivision, and homotopy invariance is shown by square subdivision, without using the imported directed van Kampen theorem.

Results

The inclusion-induced square of fundamental groupoids is proved to be a pushout in Cat for every topological space and every open cover by two sets. The proofs were machine-checked by the Palomar registry.
