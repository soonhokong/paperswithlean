Abstract
A Lean 4 formalization of p-adic valuations for the restricted binomial gcd G(N;m) = gcd{C(N,k) : 0 < k < N, m divides k}. The principal submitted result gives the complete valuation formula for primes p congruent to -1 modulo m, including the mixed-parity digit-sum branch and the exceptional p = m - 1 valuation-2 case. The repository also proves the complementary p congruent to 1 branch, a prime-power scaling reduction, and complete prime-by-prime formulas for m = 3, 4, and 6.

Problem

Determine the p-adic valuations of the restricted binomial gcd G(N;m) = gcd{C(N,k) : 0 < k < N, m divides k} for primes p in different residue classes modulo m.

Approach

All results are stated and proved in Lean 4. The main theorem handles primes p congruent to -1 modulo m, including a mixed-parity digit-sum branch and the exceptional case p = m - 1. The formalization also covers the complementary branch p congruent to 1 and a prime-power scaling reduction.

Results

The formalization gives a complete valuation formula for p congruent to -1 mod m, proves the p congruent to 1 branch and the scaling reduction, and gives complete prime-by-prime formulas for m = 3, 4, and 6.
