Delaunay Combinatorics of the Moduli and Teichmüller Space of Closed Genus-Two Riemann Surfaces

Armando Rodado Amaris

math.CO Nov 2, 2026 · v1 math.GT
formalization combinatorics geometry
Read PDF arXiv abstract
TL;DR
The finite combinatorial classification of Delaunay graph types, their automorphism groups and the flip orbit are verified in Lean 4 using native_decide.
Abstract
Every closed Riemann surface of genus two is hyperelliptic, so it is determined by six branch points on the sphere, which become cone points of angle pi in the quotient orbifold metric. The Delaunay decomposition of these cone points, dual to the Voronoi diagram of the Weierstrass points, gives a trivalent graph with eight vertices, twelve edges and six faces. We classify these graphs. When the dual triangulation is simple there are exactly two types, dual to the octahedron and to a stacked triangulation, with orientation-preserving automorphism groups of orders 24 and 2, and we determine the Whitehead-move (diagonal-flip) orbit connecting them. Allowing bigons, the orbit closes at exactly twenty types. Iterated edge contraction from the two simple types gives a seven-level hierarchy of 2, 6, 22, 32, 22, 5 and 1 types, ending at two vertices joined by six parallel edges; closing under both contraction and expansion gives all 280 maps on the sphere with six vertices and no vertex of degree one. The rank of the linear cone-angle system of each type equals the real dimension 6 of the Teichmüller space. We realize both simple types by explicit hyperbolic cone spheres, in closed form for the octahedral type, build the corresponding genus-two surfaces by cutting and doubling, and show that the boundary x_e = 0 of the angle system is exactly the concyclicity of four branch points. The classification, the automorphism groups and the flip orbit are formally verified in Lean 4, and every enumeration is reproduced by an independent program, which also determines which types are realized by Delaunay decompositions.
Problem

Closed genus-two Riemann surfaces are hyperelliptic and determined by six branch points on the sphere. The goal is to classify the combinatorial types of Delaunay decompositions of these cone points, which are trivalent graphs with eight vertices, twelve edges and six faces.

Approach

The authors enumerate the graph types, compute their orientation-preserving automorphism groups, and study Whitehead-move (diagonal-flip) orbits and iterated edge contractions. Both simple types are realized by explicit hyperbolic cone spheres, and genus-two surfaces are built from them by cutting and doubling. The finite combinatorics is formalized in Lean 4 (v4.15, core library) with native_decide, and an independent program reproduces every enumeration.

Results

There are exactly two simple types, dual to the octahedron and to a stacked triangulation, with automorphism groups of orders 24 and 2. Allowing bigons, the flip orbit closes at 20 types. Iterated contraction gives a seven-level hierarchy of 2, 6, 22, 32, 22, 5 and 1 types, and closing under contraction and expansion yields 280 maps. The rank of each type's cone-angle system equals 6, and the boundary x_e = 0 corresponds to concyclicity of four branch points.
