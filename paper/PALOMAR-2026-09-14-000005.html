Abstract
We prove sufficient conditions for partitioning the edges of a connected finite simple graph into at most ⌈|V(G)|/2⌉ simple paths. The results concern bowtie components—two triangles sharing exactly one vertex—in the subgraph induced by even-degree vertices. They include removal preserving specified endpoints, a sharp full-incidence star expansion, composition under explicit boundary hypotheses, and corollaries for graphs with two bowtie components. The unrestricted two-exception conjecture remains open.

Problem

Gallai's conjecture states that the edges of a connected finite simple graph can be partitioned into at most ⌈|V(G)|/2⌉ simple paths. The work treats graphs whose subgraph induced by even-degree vertices contains bowtie components, meaning two triangles sharing exactly one vertex.

Approach

Sufficient conditions for such path decompositions are established and formalized in Lean. The arguments cover removal of bowtie components while preserving specified endpoints, a sharp full-incidence star expansion, and composition of decompositions under explicit boundary hypotheses. The Palomar registry machine-checked the proofs.

Results

The conditions yield Gallai's bound under the stated boundary hypotheses, with corollaries for graphs having two bowtie components. The unrestricted two-exception case of the conjecture remains open.
