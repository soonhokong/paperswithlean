Abstract
A Lean formalization of the quantitative theory of advection-diffusion in self-similar random incompressible flows: the superdiffusive asymptotics of the effective diffusivity (Theorem A), the renormalization of the generator (quantitative homogenization of the Dirichlet problem at every scale, Theorem B), and the anomalous large-scale regularity theory (Theorem C) of Armstrong-Bou-Rabee-Kuusi, together with the sensitivity and coarse-matrix-derivative theory, the diffusivity recursion, and the diffusion-process theory that support them. The formalization consumes the public CoarseGraining homogenization library as its analytic base and the public MarkovProcess library for the general theory of continuous strong Markov processes of Feller semigroups. The three headline theorems are independently checked using the SuperdiffusionAudit/ comparator pairs.

Problem

The quantitative theory of advection-diffusion in self-similar random incompressible flows, due to Armstrong-Bou-Rabee-Kuusi, involves long and intricate arguments. These establish superdiffusive behavior and anomalous regularity, and they have not previously been machine-checked.

Approach

The three headline theorems are formalized in Lean. Theorem A gives the superdiffusive asymptotics of the effective diffusivity. Theorem B covers renormalization of the generator through quantitative homogenization of the Dirichlet problem at every scale. Theorem C is the anomalous large-scale regularity theory. Supporting theory is also formalized: sensitivity estimates, coarse-matrix derivatives, the diffusivity recursion, and diffusion processes. The development builds on the public CoarseGraining homogenization library and on the MarkovProcess library for continuous strong Markov processes of Feller semigroups.

Results

Theorems A, B and C are formalized and machine-checked. They are independently verified using the SuperdiffusionAudit comparator pairs.
