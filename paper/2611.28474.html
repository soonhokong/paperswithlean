On the tensor product of semistable Hermitian adelic vector bundles over an adelic curve

Huayi Chen, Yijun Yuan

Abstract
We prove tensor semistability for Hermitian adelic vector bundles on proper adelic curves whose underlying fields are countable and of characteristic zero. The central estimate concerns the canonical integral Specht lattice of a rectangular partition with fixed height: its logarithmic covolume, divided by its rank and tensor degree, tends to zero. We prove this estimate from monic seminormal norms and an exact hypergeometric law weighted by the square of a Vandermonde product for tableau prefixes. Constant adelic Specht bundles and equivariant contractions then yield the slope inequality for every geometrically semistable subspace of a finite tensor product. The correction is the measure of the Archimedean places times a sublinear Specht term and vanishes in the limit as the auxiliary degree tends to infinity. An instability-filtration argument completes the reduction to arbitrary factors and identifies the tensor product Harder–Narasimhan filtration. The number field specialization gives the Bost tensor semistability conjecture in all ranks.
Problem

Bost's conjecture asks whether tensor products of semistable Hermitian vector bundles over number fields remain semistable. The authors address the analogous question for Hermitian adelic vector bundles on proper adelic curves over countable fields of characteristic zero.

Approach

The central estimate shows that the logarithmic covolume of the integral Specht lattice of a rectangular partition with fixed height, divided by its rank and tensor degree, tends to zero. It is proved via monic seminormal norms and an exact hypergeometric law weighted by a squared Vandermonde product over tableau prefixes. Constant adelic Specht bundles and equivariant contractions give a slope inequality for geometrically semistable subspaces of tensor products. An instability-filtration argument then extends the result to arbitrary factors. The results are formalized in Lean 4 with mathlib4.

Results

Tensor semistability holds in this setting, and the tensor product Harder–Narasimhan filtration is identified. Specializing to number fields gives the Bost tensor semistability conjecture in all ranks. The Lean formalization is sorry-free and depends only on the standard axioms.
