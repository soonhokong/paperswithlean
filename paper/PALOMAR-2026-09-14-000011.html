TL;DR
Lean formalization, machine-checked by the Palomar registry, of an explicit Haar-integral evaluation and Abel limits for a Fourier–Jacobi local kernel over nonarchimedean fields.
Abstract
For a nonarchimedean local field with residue cardinality q > 2, this development evaluates a sum of three normalized Haar integrals of an explicit eight-term Weyl kernel defined from valuations of entries and two-by-two minors of a concrete 4 by 4 matrix. It proves measurability and Bochner integrability on the natural damped range, an explicit rational evaluation in the undamped interior, principal Abel convergence, and the whole signed special Abel limit at delta = plus or minus q^(-1/2). The Satake parameters are unitary and avoid the stated Weyl walls; the special quotient also excludes parameters equal to its sign. The proof assembles all fifty infinite valuation regions with the source Haar normalizations. This adapts local calculations in Paul, Pitale, Saha and Schmidt's Fourier–Jacobi period paper. Identification of the explicit kernel with the paper's independently defined spherical coefficient and the full period identity are not claimed. The positive special-sign product is identically zero. No undamped special-endpoint integrability or extension to exceptional parameter walls is asserted.

Problem

Local calculations in Paul, Pitale, Saha and Schmidt's Fourier–Jacobi period paper require evaluating Haar integrals of an explicit Weyl kernel over a nonarchimedean local field with residue cardinality q > 2. The goal is to make these computations rigorous, including their convergence behaviour.

Approach

The development defines an eight-term Weyl kernel from valuations of entries and two-by-two minors of a concrete 4 by 4 matrix. It proves measurability and Bochner integrability on the damped range. It then assembles all fifty infinite valuation regions using the source Haar normalizations to evaluate a sum of three normalized Haar integrals. Satake parameters are assumed unitary and away from the stated Weyl walls.

Results

The formalization establishes an explicit rational evaluation in the undamped interior, principal Abel convergence, and the full signed special Abel limit at delta = ±q^(-1/2); the positive special-sign product is identically zero. It does not identify the kernel with the paper's spherical coefficient, prove the full period identity, or cover undamped special-endpoint integrability or exceptional parameter walls.
