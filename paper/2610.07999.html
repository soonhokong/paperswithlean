On the number of cokernel-closed additive subcategories for uniformly oriented $A_n$ quivers

Volodymyr Mazorchuk, Shuoyang Xu

Abstract
We study the integer sequence that enumerates cokernel-closed, idempotent split, full, additive subcategories of the category of finite dimensional complex representations of a uniformly oriented $A_n$ quiver. Using a combinatorial model, we describe a non-obvious connection of this sequence to Catalan numbers and derive an implicit recurrence. We also describe some basic properties of the lattice underlying the sequences, in particular, we give an explicit description of the meet irreducible elements of that lattice.
Problem

The work enumerates cokernel-closed, idempotent split, full additive subcategories of finite-dimensional complex representations of a uniformly oriented A_n quiver. This integer sequence was identified by Krause and Stoye, and only its first terms were known.

Approach

Indecomposable representations correspond to intervals, so a subcategory becomes a subset of a triangular array of points. Cokernel closure translates into a combinatorial closure condition on these subsets. Threshold vectors are introduced and linked to a classical Catalan-number model. This yields a recurrence over threshold vectors, and the underlying lattice of closed subsets is analyzed.

Results

Implication states at level m are counted by the Catalan number C_{m+2}. An implicit recurrence gives terms of the sequence up to n=14. The lattice is atomic and atomistic with n+1 coatoms, and its meet-irreducible elements are described explicitly. The results of the original version were formalized in Lean 4 by a third party.
