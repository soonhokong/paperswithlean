Density elimination for weakly commutative pseudo-uninorm logic:a correction and an explicit theory-level completeness proof

SanMin Wang

math.LO Nov 24, 2026 · v1
formalization logic
Read PDF arXiv abstract
TL;DR

Abstract
We correct the density-elimination argument for weakly commutative pseudo-uninorm logic in Wang, Symmetry 11 (2019), 368, retaining its basic ideas and overall strategy. The published definition of a labelled copy permits independent renamings of antecedent and succedent labels. Under the syntactic splicing interpretation used in that argument, a contraction-free derivable hypersequent and a four-element residuated chain refute the claimed compatibility of the resulting contraction with ordered density elimination. We require a single renaming on both sides of each closed block and prove that the linear rules and coherent closed-block contraction preserve provability of every member of the associated Cartesian families. An occurrence-based separation argument, together with normalization to cuts on the monoidal unit, establishes strong density elimination. We then derive standard completeness from density, retaining the original conclusion for formula theorems. For an explicit Hilbert presentation with independent conjugation parameters, we give a direct relative chain-countermodel proof for arbitrary theories in a countable language. Combined with the full bounded embedding of Wang (2026), this recovers the corresponding strong standard completeness result already stated there. The distinguished constant $f$, when present, is interpreted independently of the monoidal unit and the lattice bounds. The main results of this paper have been formally verified in Lean 4.
Problem

A published density-elimination argument for weakly commutative pseudo-uninorm logic (Wang, Symmetry 2019) is flawed. Its definition of labelled copies allows independent renamings of antecedent and succedent labels, and a counterexample refutes the claimed compatibility of contraction with ordered density elimination.

Approach

Labelled copies are required to use a single renaming on both sides of each closed block. Linear rules and coherent closed-block contraction are then shown to preserve provability across the associated Cartesian families. Strong density elimination follows from an occurrence-based separation argument and normalization to cuts on the monoidal unit. Standard completeness and a relative chain-countermodel proof for arbitrary theories are derived, and the main results are formalized in Lean 4 with axiom-audit scripts.

Results

Strong density elimination and standard completeness for formula theorems are established. Combined with a bounded embedding, the work recovers strong standard completeness for an explicit Hilbert presentation. The Lean 4 formalization is archived as GpsUL-1.0.0 on Zenodo, with a map from manuscript statements to Lean declarations.
