TL;DR
Lean 4 formalization over Mathlib of an infinite simple group with property (T) that is sofic and hyperlinear, using only the standard axioms propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound.
Abstract
Brown asks in 2001 (arXiv:math/0111286, Question 11.7) whether an infinite simple discrete group with Kazhdan's property (T) can embed in the unitary group of an R^ω-embeddable McDuff factor, which for such a group is hyperlinearity; Ozawa asks it in 2003 in that language, in About the QWEP conjecture (arXiv:math/0306067); and Pestov prints it, with the sofic form added, as Open question 9.1 of Hyperlinear and sofic groups: a brief guide (arXiv:0804.3968): can an infinite simple group with Kazhdan's property (T) be hyperlinear, or sofic? This project proves, in Lean 4 over Mathlib, that the answer is yes in both forms. The witness is EL₃(A), the group generated by the elementary matrices of size three over the ring A = LC(X, 𝔽₂) ⋊ ℤ, the algebraic crossed product of the locally constant 𝔽₂-valued functions on a binary Toeplitz subshift X by the shift. The ring is finitely generated and simple with center 𝔽₂; the group is infinite and simple, has property (T) by the theorem of Ershov and Jaikin-Zapirain, proved here, and is locally embeddable into finite groups by periodic models of the Toeplitz sequence, so it is sofic and hyperlinear. The two compared theorems state the existence of an infinite simple group with property (T) that is hyperlinear, and of one that is sofic, with property (T), soficity and hyperlinearity defined in the Challenge from the textbook sources; their axiom closure is propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound. Problem

Brown (2001), Ozawa (2003) and Pestov (Open question 9.1) asked whether an infinite simple group with Kazhdan's property (T) can be hyperlinear or sofic. The problem is to answer both forms of this question.

Approach

The witness group is EL₃(A), generated by the 3×3 elementary matrices over A = LC(X, 𝔽₂) ⋊ ℤ. A is the algebraic crossed product of the locally constant 𝔽₂-valued functions on a binary Toeplitz subshift X by the shift. A is shown to be finitely generated and simple with center 𝔽₂. Property (T) follows from the Ershov–Jaikin-Zapirain theorem, which is itself proved in Lean, and local embeddability into finite groups comes from periodic models of the Toeplitz sequence.

Results

Lean 4 theorems over Mathlib establish that an infinite simple group with property (T) exists that is hyperlinear, and one exists that is sofic, answering the question in both forms. The definitions follow textbook sources, and the proofs rely only on the axioms propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound.
