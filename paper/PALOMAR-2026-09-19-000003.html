Abstract
A Lean 4 / Mathlib formalization of the Brydges-Kennedy-Abdesselam-Rivasseau forest interpolation formula: for a finite vertex set V and a C-infinity function rho on the edge-coupling space of the complete graph on V, the value of rho at the all-ones coupling equals the sum, over all acyclic edge subsets (forests) F, of the integral over the unit cube [0,1]^E(F) of the mixed partial derivative of rho in the edge variables of F, evaluated at the path-minimum interpolation point x^F(u); the empty forest contributes rho(0). The flagship theorem is additionally restated Mathlib-only and re-proved in the leanprover/comparator challenge/solution format using Audit/ForestFormula/comparator.json.

Problem

The BKAR forest formula is an interpolation identity used in constructive field theory and cluster expansions. The task is to give it a machine-checked proof.

Approach

The formalization works over a finite vertex set V and a smooth function rho on the edge-coupling space of the complete graph on V. It proves that rho at the all-ones coupling equals a sum over all forests F. Each term is the integral over [0,1]^E(F) of the mixed partial derivative of rho in the edges of F, evaluated at the path-minimum interpolation point x^F(u). The main theorem is also restated using only Mathlib and re-proved in the leanprover/comparator challenge/solution format.

Results

A complete Lean 4 proof of the forest formula, with the empty forest contributing rho(0), which the Palomar registry machine-checked. A comparator audit configuration (comparator.json) checks the Mathlib-only statement.
