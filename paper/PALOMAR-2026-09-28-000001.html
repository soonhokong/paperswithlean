Problem

Global class field theory states that for a finite abelian extension L/K of number fields, the idèle class group C_K modulo the norm image from C_L is isomorphic to Gal(L/K). The goal is a machine-checked Lean statement and proof of this reciprocity theorem.

Approach

The challenge statement defines the idèle-class norm as the determinant on the relative adèle algebra and descends it through principal idèles. An accompanying ClassFieldTheory development in Lean 4 supplies the proof of the resulting reciprocity isomorphism as topological multiplicative groups.

Results

A Lean formalization of the global reciprocity isomorphism C_K / N(C_L) ≅ Gal(L/K), with proofs machine-checked by the Palomar registry.
