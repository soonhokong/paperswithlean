Free Products of Vector Lattices

Gonzalo Martínez-Fernández, Pedro Tradacete

Abstract
We study free products of vector lattices and their relation to free products of Banach lattices. We characterize the Archimedean vector lattices whose vector lattice free product with every Archimedean vector lattice is again Archimedean: these are precisely those whose finitely generated sublattices are projective. For finitely generated lattices, this connects the free-product property with polyhedral geometry through Baker–Beynon duality. We also show that the Banach lattice free product is the completion of the Archimedean vector lattice free product under the largest lattice norm making the canonical maps contractive. For finitely many factors, the Archimedean vector lattice free product is order dense in the Banach lattice free product. Moreover, it is shown that bounded families of injective lattice homomorphisms also induce injective homomorphisms between Banach lattice free products. Finally, in analogy with the theory of separably injective Banach lattices, we study relative injectivity and universal disposition for the class of vector lattices with countably many proper prime ideals. Every vector lattice embeds into one of universal disposition for this class, while ordered ultrapowers of the real line provide relatively injective examples of cardinality continuum.
Problem

Free products of vector lattices and their relation to Banach lattice free products are not well understood. Open questions include when Archimedean free products remain Archimedean and how the algebraic and Banach constructions relate.

Approach

Free products are constructed in the categories of vector lattices, Archimedean vector lattices and Banach lattices. They are analyzed using Archimedeanization, the join representation of free products of C(K) spaces, Baker–Beynon duality, and amalgamation arguments. The numbered statements are machine-checked in Lean 4 using Mathlib and the BanLat library, and the build checks that only Lean's standard axioms are used.

Results

An Archimedean vector lattice has an Archimedean free product with every Archimedean vector lattice exactly when its finitely generated sublattices are projective. The Banach lattice free product is the completion of the Archimedean free product under the maximal compatible lattice norm, and bounded families of injective homomorphisms induce injective maps. Every vector lattice embeds into one of universal disposition for the class with countable spectrum, and ordered ultrapowers of the real line give relatively injective examples of cardinality continuum.
