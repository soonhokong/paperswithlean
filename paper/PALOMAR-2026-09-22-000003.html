Abstract
A Lean 4 / Mathlib formalization of the Caffarelli-Kohn-Nirenberg partial regularity theory for suitable weak solutions of the three-dimensional incompressible Navier-Stokes equations with forcing in L^q, q > 5/2: the L^3 epsilon-regularity theorem (Theorem A), the gradient epsilon-regularity criterion (Theorem B) and the vanishing of the one-dimensional parabolic Hausdorff measure of the singular set (Theorem C). The three theorems are proved using only the standard axioms, restated together in a Mathlib-only comparator challenge and proved from the library theorems. The manuscript being formalized is in the repository and was corrected alongside the development.

Problem

The Caffarelli-Kohn-Nirenberg partial regularity theory for suitable weak solutions of the 3D incompressible Navier-Stokes equations is a deep result in PDE analysis. It had not been machine-checked. The target setting allows forcing in L^q with q > 5/2.

Approach

The theory is formalized in Lean 4 on top of Mathlib, covering three results. Theorem A is the L^3 epsilon-regularity theorem, Theorem B is the gradient epsilon-regularity criterion, and Theorem C states that the one-dimensional parabolic Hausdorff measure of the singular set vanishes. The three theorems are restated together in a Mathlib-only comparator challenge and proved from the library's theorems. The source manuscript is kept in the repository and was corrected alongside the formal development.

Results

All three theorems are proved using only the standard axioms. The proofs were machine-checked by the Palomar registry. Errors in the manuscript were corrected in the course of formalization.
