Problem

Davis and Kahan (1970) state four families of perturbation bounds for how far eigenvectors rotate: sin-Theta, tan-Theta, sin-2-Theta and tan-2-Theta. These bounds had not been machine-checked in their original generality: separable Hilbert spaces of finite or infinite dimension, and arbitrary unitarily invariant norms.

Approach

The four Section 2 theorem families are stated and proved in Lean 4, generically over RCLike scalars so that both the real and complex cases are covered. The norm quantifier is expressed through a dimension-coherent symmetric norming function. Fan dominance is used to derive estimates that hold for every unitarily invariant norm. A Palomar Comparator exposes five source-facing declarations: the residual sin-Theta, residual tan-Theta, residual and whole-space sin-2-Theta, and residual tan-2-Theta estimates.

Results

The Palomar registry machine-checked Lean proofs of all five selected declarations. The proofs keep the paper's separable Hilbert-space scope and yield the universal unitarily invariant norm estimates.
