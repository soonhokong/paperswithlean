Robust Strictly Positive Real Synthesis for Sixth-Order Interval Polynomial Families

Tiancheng Xu, Guowei Duo, Xiang Ji, Long Wang, Wensheng Yu

eess.SY Sep 22, 2026 · v1
formalization verification algebra
Read PDF arXiv abstract Code
TL;DR
The complete existence theorem for equal-degree robust SPR synthesis of sixth-order interval polynomial families is formalized and machine-checked in Lean 4 with Mathlib.
Abstract
Every Hurwitz-stable interval family of monic real polynomials of degree six admits a single real numerator of degree six that makes all the associated transfer functions strictly positive real. We give a constructive proof. The complete existence theorem has been formalized in Lean4.
Problem

For a Hurwitz-stable interval family of monic real degree-six polynomials, one must find a single fixed real numerator of degree six making every associated transfer function strictly positive real. Robust SPR synthesis requires a common numerator solving infinitely many frequency inequalities across all admissible denominators.

Approach

A constructive proof reduces the frequency inequalities to four endpoint combinations via Kharitonov-style analysis. The numerator is built from root geometry of the even endpoint polynomials, with quadratic bounds on the odd components, interpolation-based corrections, and polynomial certificates establishing nonnegativity of four auxiliary polynomials. Strict positivity is obtained from strict odd-part separation. The complete existence theorem, including robust Hurwitz stability and strict positivity at every frequency, is formalized in Lean 4.30.0 with Mathlib v4.30.0.

Results

Robust Hurwitz stability is proven necessary and sufficient for a common equal-degree monic SPR numerator for sextic interval families. The public Lean theorem SPR.N6.Direct.robustSPR depends only on propext, Classical.choice, and Quot.sound, with finite coefficient certificate calculations checked inside Lean.
