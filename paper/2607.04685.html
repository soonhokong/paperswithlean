Classification of $σ$-validity in iterated announcements

Eiji Yamada

cs.LO Jul 6, 2026 · v3
formalization logic
Read PDF arXiv abstract Code
TL;DR
Provides a Lean formalization of the σ-validity classification results for iterated believed public announcements, available in a GitHub repository.
Abstract
In their 2018 paper, Agotnes, van Ditmarsch, and Wang extended the notions of success and self-refutation in public announcements to true lies, impossible lies, and $σ$-validity in general. Here, $σ$ is a finite or infinite sequence of $0$s and $1$s. For example, successful formulas and self-refuting formulas are $11$-valid and $10$-valid, respectively. They then posed a conjecture on the classification of such sequences in terms of $σ$-validity. In this paper, we disprove the conjecture and give corrected classifications for multi-agent K45, single-agent KD45, multi-agent KD45 with more than one agent, and multi-agent S5 after reformulating the statement more explicitly. The results indicate that there is an asymmetry between truthful announcements and false announcements: the former are stable while the latter are fragile in general. In particular, all successful formulas remain true forever while some impossible lies can be true at some point when repeatedly announced. Also, although some self-refuting formulas can become true again after following the truth pattern $10$, all $100$-valid formulas are destructive in the sense that they remain false forever once they become false. On the other hand, some true lies are fragile in the sense that truths created by lying can become false again.
Problem

Agotnes, van Ditmarsch, and Wang conjectured a classification of the 0/1 sequences σ for which non-trivially σ-valid formulas exist under iterated believed public announcements. The conjecture covers successful, self-refuting, true-lie, and impossible-lie formulas.

Approach

The conjecture is restated explicitly in terms of equivalence classes of sequences with identical sets of valid formulas. Collapse lemmas are proved, for example that 11-validity implies 1^ω-validity and that 00-validity implies 0^ω-validity in K45. Explicit formulas are constructed to witness the remaining classes. The results are formalized in Lean.

Results

The conjecture is disproved. Corrected classifications are given for multi-agent K45, single-agent KD45, multi-agent KD45 with at least two agents, and multi-agent S5. They show an asymmetry: truthful announcements are stable, while truths created by false announcements are fragile.
