Uniform Bessel bounds for magnetization crossings in finite Ising chains

Arjun Pemmasani

math.PR Oct 3, 2026 · v2 cond-mat.stat-mech

TL;DR
Many results, including the every-N crossing bracket (Theorem 3.4), are formalized in Lean 4, with the kernel checking integer certificates for N≤174.
Abstract
In a finite zero-field Ising chain, the all-up configuration and zero magnetization become equally likely at a single coupling. This crossing has recently been described as a thermal transition of the finite chain, an analogue of a first-order transition temperature, and approximated by an analytic estimate and a numerical fit. We find its asymptotics, with explicit constants, for free and for periodic ends. The coupling at the crossing grows like half the logarithm of the length of the chain, minus half the logarithm of that logarithm. So the correlation length at the crossing is a vanishing fraction of the chain. Periodic ends lower the coupling at the crossing by an amount that decays like the inverse logarithm of the length. The earlier approximations lack the doubly logarithmic term, which explains why a fit on chains of moderate length finds a slope below one half. The proofs treat the magnetization as the position of a persistent random walk and compare its central probability with a modified Bessel function, with a relative error bound that is explicit for every length. For free ends this traps the crossing in an explicit interval at every length. The proof is analytic for long chains and uses exact integer arithmetic for short ones. We also locate, for each smaller magnetization, the coupling at which it becomes as likely as full magnetization.
Problem

In a finite zero-field Ising chain (equivalently a persistent random walk or Galton board with momentum), the all-up configuration and zero magnetization become equally likely at a single coupling. The goal is to find precise asymptotics with explicit constants for this crossing, for free and periodic boundaries, answering Kagey's Problem 131.

Approach

The magnetization is treated as the position of a persistent random walk. Its central probability is compared with modified Bessel functions I_0 and I_1, using relative error bounds that are explicit for every chain length. Long chains are handled analytically. Short chains (N≤174) are handled with exact integer-arithmetic certificates, which are re-checked in Lean 4 along with many of the theorems.

Results

The crossing satisfies N(1-p_N) = log N - ½ log log N + c_0 + o(1) and is trapped in an explicit interval for every N≥2. Periodic ends lower the crossing coupling by an amount that decays like the inverse logarithm of the length. Crossings are also located for every bin, and Lean 4 formalizations using only the standard axioms are provided for most main results.
