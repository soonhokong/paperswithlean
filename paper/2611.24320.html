Only the disk: topological obstructions for warped AdS$_6\times S^2$ in Type IIB

Nakwoo Kim

hep-th Nov 18, 2026 · v1
formalization mathlib physics geometry
Read PDF arXiv abstract Code
TL;DR
Counting steps of the main theorems, pointwise identities and a boundary rotation bound were formalized in Lean 4 with Mathlib; files ship as ancillary files.
Abstract
Supersymmetric AdS$_6\times S^2$ solutions of Type IIB supergravity are warped products over a Riemann surface $Σ$. They are determined by two holomorphic differentials on $Σ$, with five-branes at poles on its boundary. We derive restrictions on the genus and the number of boundary components of $Σ$. Without seven-branes or orientifolds, the differentials are taken to be single-valued on $Σ$. Regularity implies that the number of interior zeros of one differential equals the total rotation of the phase of the ratio of the two differentials along the boundary. It also bounds this rotation on each boundary component by the number of poles on that component. Combined with the known count of zeros on the double of $Σ$, these two statements force $Σ$ to be a disk. With mutually local seven-branes and orientifolds as interior punctures, the same conclusion follows under additional assumptions on the global structure of the differentials and on their behavior at the punctures. The disk then admits at most one orientifold puncture. On a disk without punctures, the bound applied between successive poles also recovers the known cyclic ordering of the five-brane charges along the boundary.
Problem

Supersymmetric warped AdS6×S2 solutions of Type IIB supergravity are fibered over a Riemann surface Σ with five-brane poles on its boundary. The question is which genus and how many boundary components Σ can have.

Approach

Regularity and positivity of κ² and of the superharmonic function G are used to bound the phase rotation of the ratio of the two holomorphic differentials along each boundary component. The bound is then combined with the degree formula for meromorphic one-forms on the Schottky double. The argument is extended to mutually local seven-branes and orientifold punctures through a monodromy-invariant differential. Counting steps and several local statements were checked in Lean 4 with Mathlib, with the code written by Claude Code.

Results

Without monodromy, regularity forces 2g+ν<2, so Σ must be a disk. With seven-branes or orientifolds, the disk conclusion follows under additional assumptions, and at most one orientifold puncture is allowed. On an unpunctured disk, the same bound recovers the cyclic ordering of the five-brane charges.
