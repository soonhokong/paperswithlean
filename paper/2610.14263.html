Abstract
Under the conditional framework breaking the longstanding O(n log n) integer multiplication barrier (originating from OpenAI Preprint #109), the asymptotic time complexity exponent T(n) = O(n (log n)^{1-κ}) is governed by bounding the second moment E[X^2] < 1 of recursive branching processes over trilinear tensor carrier graphs. In this work, we present a constructive combinatorial optimization over the alternating carrier bipartite matching graphs of the Schönhage trilinear tensor decomposition at dimensions h=23 and h=25. Through an alternating augmenting path search, we uncover a non-trivial cluster-unlocking cascade that eliminates 185 carrier roles (95 roles in h=23 and 90 roles in h=25), reducing the global physical wire width by 377,890 wires from the reviewed baseline W = 177,284,805 down to W = 176,906,915. All 47 polynomial constraints and 7 rational security margins are audited in exact Q-arithmetic with zero floating-point error, and the fundamental wire reduction identity is formally verified in the Lean 4 interactive theorem prover without unproven axioms.
Problem

In a conditional framework for beating the O(n log n) integer multiplication bound, the exponent κ depends inversely on the wire width W of carrier graphs in Schönhage trilinear tensor decompositions at h=23 and h=25. The goal is to reduce W below the previous record of 177,284,805 and so sharpen κ.

Approach

A 15-round greedy augmenting-path search works on the bipartite carrier matching graphs. It evaluates tensor-parity and vertex-permutation swap moves and recomputes maximum matchings with Hopcroft–Karp. A move is accepted only if it strictly reduces role counts and preserves CRT consistency and rank-mass conservation. The second-moment parameter is then bisected over an exact rational grid. The wire-width identity and the strict-improvement inequalities are formalized in Lean 4 and proved with norm_num.

Results

The search eliminates 185 carrier roles (95 at h=23, 90 at h=25), reducing W by 377,890 to 176,906,915. This certifies κ = 826,215,307/(2×10^13) ≈ 4.131077×10^-5, above the prior κ ≈ 4.123864×10^-5. The Lean theorems are fully proven without sorry.
