Problem

The Shapley value is classically characterized as the unique value on finite transferable-utility cooperative games satisfying efficiency, symmetry, the null-player property, and additivity. The goal is a reusable machine-checked version of this characterization.

Approach

Finite TU cooperative games, the Shapley value formula, and the four axioms are defined formally in Lean. The proof first shows that the Shapley value satisfies each axiom. Uniqueness is then proved by decomposing games into unanimity games using Möbius inversion over the Boolean lattice of coalitions.

Results

Both existence (the Shapley value satisfies all four axioms) and uniqueness are fully formalized, and the registry machine-checked the proofs. The work is presented as a reusable formalization artifact rather than new mathematics.
