Exponential Convergence of the Lifted Gradient Flow of the Bass Functional

Danila Litvinov

math.PR Oct 29, 2026 · v2 q-fin.MF
formalization mathlib probability analysis
Read PDF arXiv abstract
TL;DR
The final multidimensional exponential convergence theorem is formalized in Lean 4 with Mathlib, using only standard axioms.
Abstract
How quickly can we construct the martingale with prescribed initial and terminal laws that is closest to Brownian motion in the sense of the martingale Benamou-Brenier problem? The construction requires the initial law of an auxiliary Brownian motion. One can take this law to be the distribution of a minimizer of the lifted Bass functional. In dimension one, Backhoff, Pammer and Schachermayer proved, under additional assumptions, that the gradient flow of this functional converges exponentially to a minimizer. We extend this result to arbitrary dimension. Under the assumptions of the paper, the gradient flow starting from a bounded random vector converges exponentially in $L^2$ to the minimizer with the same mean. We prove this by showing that, for a fixed random vector $X$ with the prescribed initial law, the lifted Bass functional is strongly convex on each set of $σ(X)$-measurable vectors with a prescribed mean and a common $L^\infty$ bound.
Problem

Constructing the martingale with prescribed initial and terminal laws that is closest to Brownian motion requires the minimizer of the lifted Bass functional. Exponential convergence of its gradient flow was previously known only in dimension one.

Approach

The authors prove that, for a fixed random vector X with the prescribed initial law, the lifted Bass functional is strongly convex on sets of σ(X)-measurable vectors that share a prescribed mean and a common L∞ bound. Exponential convergence of the gradient flow follows from this strong convexity. The final statement is formalized in Lean 4 using Mathlib, with AI assistance in preparing the Lean code.

Results

In arbitrary dimension, the gradient flow started from a bounded random vector converges exponentially in L² to the minimizer with the same mean. The Lean declarations depend only on the axioms propext, Classical.choice, and Quot.sound.
