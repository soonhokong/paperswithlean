The regionally proximal relation for commutative semigroup actions

Angelina Blahodatna, Lauren Detmold, Daniel Glasscock, Anh N. Le

math.DS Oct 7, 2026 · v2
formalization topology

TL;DR
All results on regionally proximal relations for commutative semigroup actions are formally verified in Lean, with code in a GitHub repository.
Abstract
The regionally proximal and equicontinuous structure relations are fundamental relations in topological dynamics that capture the equicontinuous behavior in a topological dynamical system and its factors. For minimal actions of abelian groups, these relations are known to be equivalence relations that coincide. In this paper, we generalize these facts to semigroup actions: for minimal actions of commutative semigroups, the regionally proximal and equicontinuous structure relations are equivalence relations and the two coincide. We leverage our approach to prove a refinement: the regionally proximal relation for such actions is equal to an analogue and strengthening of Veech's D relation. We also develop the machinery of natural extensions for commutative, not-necessarily-cancellative semigroup actions that act by surjections, concluding that the maximal equicontinuous factor of a minimal action of a commutative semigroup is the same as the maximal equicontinuous factor of its natural extension. We formally verify all of the results in this paper in Lean. The main results are registered on Palomar, and we link to a Github repository containing code for the complete verification.
Problem

For minimal actions of abelian groups, the regionally proximal and equicontinuous structure relations are known to be equivalence relations that coincide. The work asks whether the same holds for minimal actions of commutative semigroups.

Approach

The regionally proximal relation RP and a backward variant RP^- are defined for semigroup actions. A corner space, a simple instance of Host-Kra cubes with an S^2-action on X^3, is used to study uniformly recurrent points. Natural extensions are developed for commutative, not-necessarily-cancellative semigroups acting by surjections. Ultrafilters replace enveloping semigroups and nets to simplify the Lean verification.

Results

For minimal commutative semigroup actions, RP and the equicontinuous structure relation are equivalence relations and coincide, and RP equals a strengthened analogue of Veech's D relation. The maximal equicontinuous factor agrees with that of the natural extension. All results are verified in Lean, and the main results are registered on Palomar.
