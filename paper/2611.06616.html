Vertex Operator Algebras in Lean: a formal library and its companion book

Fedor K. Popov, Leonardo Rastelli

TL;DR
Presents LeanVOA, a Lean 4/Mathlib library formalizing vertex operator algebra theory, including the Moonshine module, Borcherds' moonshine theorem, and new rigidity results.
Abstract
We present LeanVOA, a Lean 4 library for the theory of vertex operator algebras (VOAs), together with a companion book that sets each formalized result next to its textbook statement. The library constructs the Moonshine module $V^\natural$ and proves, with no hypotheses, the Conway-Norton moonshine conjecture for every automorphism of $V^\natural$ (Borcherds' theorem), along Borcherds' route through the Monster Lie algebra. It identifies $\mathrm{Aut}\,V^\natural$ with a formal model of the Monster taken from the AxiomMath library for the classification of finite simple groups, given one named hypothesis that is a theorem of that library, so that moonshine holds for every element of the Monster. It also formalizes Zhu's modular invariance, Huang's tensor categories with the Verlinde formula, the Gannon-Rayhaun classification of $c=1$ VOAs, and classical freeness of the boundary admissible $L_k(\mathfrak{sl}_2)$ (Arakawa-Dai) and of the minimal models $M(2,2m+3)$ (van Ekeren-Heluani). It also contains new results: graded first-order rigidity of all Virasoro minimal models and of $L_k(\mathfrak{sl}_2)$ at every admissible level; classical freeness of $L_{-3/2}(\mathfrak{sl}_3)$, and of the other members of the Deligne series under stated assumptions; and a proof that graded unitarity of the boundary admissible $L_k(\mathfrak{sl}_2)$ implies that of the minimal models $M(2,2n+1)$. The library declares no axiom of its own. This note announces release v1.05.
Problem

Vertex operator algebra theory, including monstrous moonshine and modern classification and freeness results, had no machine-checked formal library.

Approach

LeanVOA builds, in Lean 4 on Mathlib, formal calculus and locality from first principles, the VOA axioms, and the standard constructions: Heisenberg, Virasoro, affine and lattice. It follows Borcherds' route through the Monster Lie algebra and the no-ghost theorem to prove moonshine for automorphisms of V^natural. It identifies Aut V^natural with the Monster model from the AxiomMath Lean library for the classification of finite simple groups, under one named hypothesis that is a theorem of that library. A companion book sets each formalized result next to its textbook statement.

Results

The library formalizes the Conway-Norton conjecture for every automorphism of V^natural, Zhu's modular invariance, Huang's tensor categories with the Verlinde formula, and the classification of c=1 VOAs. It also proves new results, including graded first-order rigidity of the Virasoro minimal models and classical freeness of L_{-3/2}(sl_3). It declares no axioms of its own and uses only Lean's three standard axioms.
