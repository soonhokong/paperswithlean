Vertex Operator Algebras in Lean: a formal library and its companion book

Fedor K. Popov, Leonardo Rastelli

math.QA Nov 4, 2026 · v5 hep-th
formalization mathlib algebra
Read PDF arXiv abstract Code
TL;DR
Presents LeanVOA, a Lean 4 library built on Mathlib formalizing vertex operator algebras, the Moonshine module, Borcherds' moonshine theorem, and related results.
Abstract
We present LeanVOA, a Lean 4 library for the theory of vertex operator algebras (VOAs), together with a companion book that sets each formalized result next to its textbook statement. The library constructs the Moonshine module $V^\natural$ and proves, with no hypotheses, the Conway-Norton moonshine conjecture for every automorphism of $V^\natural$ (Borcherds' theorem), along Borcherds' route through the Monster Lie algebra. It identifies $\mathrm{Aut}\,V^\natural$ with a formal model of the Monster taken from the AxiomMath library for the classification of finite simple groups. A separate package in the repository imports that library's generation theorem for the model and proves, with no hypotheses, that $\mathrm{Aut}\,V^\natural$ is isomorphic to the model, compatibly with the Griess algebras, and that moonshine holds for every element of the Monster. It also formalizes Zhu's modular invariance, Huang's tensor categories with the Verlinde formula, the Gannon-Rayhaun classification of $c=1$ VOAs, and classical freeness of the boundary admissible $L_k(\mathfrak{sl}_2)$ (Arakawa-Dai) and of the minimal models $M(2,2m+3)$ (van Ekeren-Heluani). It also contains new results: graded first-order rigidity of all Virasoro minimal models and of $L_k(\mathfrak{sl}_2)$ at every admissible level; classical freeness of $L_{-3/2}(\mathfrak{sl}_3)$, and of the other members of the Deligne series under stated assumptions; and a proof that graded unitarity of the boundary admissible $L_k(\mathfrak{sl}_2)$ implies that of the minimal models $M(2,2n+1)$. The library declares no axiom of its own. This note announces release v1.1, which moves the whole library to Lean v4.35.
Problem

Vertex operator algebra theory, including monstrous moonshine and modern classification and rigidity results, has had no machine-checked foundation.

Approach

LeanVOA builds VOA theory in Lean 4 on top of Mathlib, starting from formal calculus and locality. It covers the VOA axioms, the standard constructions, module theory, Zhu's algebra, and Huang's tensor categories, and it constructs the Moonshine module. Borcherds' proof of moonshine is followed through the Monster Lie algebra. The Monster model is taken from the AxiomMath Lean library, and a companion book places each formal result next to its textbook statement.

Results

The library proves the Conway-Norton moonshine conjecture for every automorphism of V^natural without hypotheses, and identifies Aut V^natural with the Monster model. It also formalizes Zhu's modular invariance, the Verlinde formula, and the c=1 classification, and contains new rigidity and classical-freeness results. It declares no axioms beyond Lean's three standard ones.
