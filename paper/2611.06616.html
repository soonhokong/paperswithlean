Vertex Operator Algebras in Lean: a formal library and its companion book

Fedor K. Popov, Leonardo Rastelli

math.QA Nov 4, 2026 · v2 hep-th
formalization mathlib algebra ai-agents
Read PDF arXiv abstract Code
TL;DR
Builds LeanVOA, a Lean 4/Mathlib library formalizing vertex operator algebra theory, including the Moonshine module, Zhu's modular invariance and new rigidity results.
Abstract
We present LeanVOA, a Lean 4 library for the theory of vertex operator algebras (VOAs), together with a companion book that sets each formalized result next to its textbook statement. The library constructs the Moonshine module $V^\natural$ and formalizes Zhu's modular invariance, Huang's tensor categories with the Verlinde formula, the Gannon-Rayhaun classification of $c=1$ VOAs, and classical freeness of the boundary admissible $L_k(\mathfrak{sl}_2)$ (Arakawa-Dai) and of the minimal models $M(2,2m+3)$ (van Ekeren-Heluani). It also contains new results: graded first-order rigidity of all Virasoro minimal models and of $L_k(\mathfrak{sl}_2)$ at every admissible level; classical freeness of $L_{-3/2}(\mathfrak{sl}_3)$, and of the other members of the Deligne series under stated assumptions; and a proof that graded unitarity of the boundary admissible $L_k(\mathfrak{sl}_2)$ implies that of the minimal models $M(2,2n+1)$. The library declares no axiom of its own.
Problem

Vertex operator algebra theory, with results such as the Moonshine module, Zhu's modular invariance, Huang's tensor categories and recent classification theorems, had no machine-checked formal treatment.

Approach

The authors built LeanVOA, a Lean 4 library on Mathlib that develops VOAs of CFT type from formal calculus and locality, then the standard constructions, module theory, Zhu's algebra and tensor categories. AI coding agents (Claude, Codex) wrote the Lean code under human direction. Python-generated certificate data is re-checked in the kernel through decide +kernel. A 2,337-page companion book sets each Lean declaration verbatim beside its textbook statement, with AI and human referee audits.

Results

The library has about 3.75M lines in 11,660 files and declares no custom axioms, sorry or native_decide. It constructs V^natural with graded dimensions matching j(τ)−744 and formalizes the Gannon–Rayhaun c=1 classification, Arakawa–Dai and van Ekeren–Heluani classical freeness, and the Verlinde formula. It also contains new results: graded first-order rigidity of the Virasoro minimal models and of L_k(sl_2) at every admissible level, and classical freeness of L_{-3/2}(sl_3).
