Abstract
Formalizes in Lean 4 the resolution of a 2019 OEIS conjecture of L. H. Gallardo (comment on A071642; TheoremDB P12771): for every n ≥ 2 the all-ones polynomial 1 + X + ⋯ + Xⁿ is irreducible over GF(2) if and only if 1 + P + ⋯ + Pⁿ is irreducible over GF(2), where P = X² + X + 1. The compared statements are the per-n equivalence, the literal set-level form of the OEIS comment (the terms ≥ 2 coincide), and the failure of the excluded case n = 1. The proof is elementary: a factorization of the all-ones polynomial composes to one of the substituted polynomial; conversely irreducibility forces n even, the X^(n-1) coefficient gives trace 1 for a root α, hence Tr(α + 1) = 1, so Z² + Z + (α + 1) has no root in GF(2^n) (Frobenius-invariance of the trace), a root β of it satisfies P(β) = α and has degree 2n, and the monic degree-2n polynomial 1 + P + ⋯ + Pⁿ vanishing at β is its minimal polynomial. SCOPE. The full conjecture is formalized with no weakened hypothesis. NOT CLAIMED: novelty of the mechanism, which is the classical trace criterion for compositions f(X² + X + c) over binary fields (Varshamov 1984, Meyn 1990); the contribution is the resolution of the specific OEIS conjecture and its machine check. AUDIENCE: finite fields and integer sequences.

Problem

A 2019 OEIS conjecture by L. H. Gallardo (A071642) states that for n ≥ 2, the all-ones polynomial 1 + X + ⋯ + Xⁿ is irreducible over GF(2) if and only if 1 + P + ⋯ + Pⁿ is irreducible, where P = X² + X + 1.

Approach

The Lean 4 development proves three statements: the equivalence for each n, the set-level form of the OEIS comment, and the failure of the excluded case n = 1. One direction composes a factorization of the all-ones polynomial into a factorization of the substituted polynomial. The converse first shows irreducibility forces n to be even. It then uses the X^(n-1) coefficient to get trace 1 for a root α, and Frobenius-invariance of the trace to show Z² + Z + (α+1) has no root in GF(2^n). A root β of that quadratic then has degree 2n, which identifies the substituted polynomial as β's minimal polynomial.

Results

The full conjecture is formalized with no weakened hypotheses, and the registry machine-checked the proofs. The underlying mechanism is the classical trace criterion (Varshamov 1984, Meyn 1990), so the contribution is the resolution of this specific conjecture and its machine check.
