Abstract
Exact height-span of Mazur's scheme grammar, a local dart-mass bound, and an adjugate identity for the Dirichlet matrix of that construction; not a proof of the strong Papadimitriou–Ratajczak conjecture and not a theorem that every 3-connected plane graph attains the scheme maximum. Mazur builds injective integer heights by inductive recurrences on rooted pieces (edge, cycle, binary series, two-range join) and then forms a weighted Dirichlet matrix for the horizontal coordinate; the paper does not bound the height span or the bit length of the coordinates. For a well-formed scheme on n >= 2 vertices with j nontrivial joins, T+1 <= 2^j (n-2j), attained at every admissible pair (n,j)=(2,0) or 2j+3 <= n. The one-parameter maximum is F(n) = 2^{n/2}-1 (n even) or 3 * 2^{(n-3)/2}-1 (n odd). The coarser bound T <= 2^{n-2} remains. An integer matrix has |det| at most the product of its row ell_1-norms. Heights in {0,...,T} force Mazur's interior dart weights to be at most (n-1)T, so every Dirichlet row has ell_1-norm at most 2(n-1)^2 T when T >= 1, and |det M| <= (2(n-1)^2 T)^n; locally the interior outgoing dart-weight sum is at most a_s b_s T. Combining the coarser span bound with the row-sum bound sizes T^2 |det M| and T^2 |det M| T. Independently of invertibility, under the height bound h i <= T, z = adj(M) u satisfies Mz = Delta u and z_s = Delta h_s (T-h_s) on the boundary as ordinary integer subtraction. Polynomial-area Euclidean planar greedy drawings of all 3-connected planar graphs remain open (Da Lozzo–D'Angelo–Frati, COCOON 2020).

Problem

Mazur's greedy-drawing construction for 3-connected plane graphs builds injective integer heights via inductive scheme recurrences and a weighted Dirichlet matrix. It gives no bound on the height span or on the bit length of the resulting coordinates.

Approach

Heights are analyzed over a scheme grammar of rooted pieces: edge, cycle, binary series and two-range join. The span is bounded in terms of the number of vertices and nontrivial joins. Dart weights and Dirichlet row ℓ1-norms are bounded from the height range, and a determinant bound follows from a row-norm product inequality. An adjugate identity is established without assuming invertibility.

Results

For n ≥ 2 vertices and j nontrivial joins, T+1 ≤ 2^j(n−2j), attained at every admissible (n,j). The one-parameter maximum F(n) is determined exactly, and |det M| ≤ (2(n−1)^2 T)^n. The adjugate solution z = adj(M)u satisfies Mz = Δu with explicit boundary values. The strong Papadimitriou–Ratajczak conjecture and polynomial-area greedy drawings are not resolved.
