An explicit refined Gan–Gross–Prasad identity for Fourier–Jacobi periods of degree 2 Siegel cusp forms

Biplab Paul, Ameya Pitale, Abhishek Saha, Ralf Schmidt

math.NT Aug 26, 2026 · v2
formalization number-theory
Read PDF arXiv abstract
TL;DR
Local integral computations from Section 2 (Macdonald formula evaluations) are formalized and machine-verified in Lean 4, with a public GitHub repository.
Abstract
We compute the local integrals appearing in the refined Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture for Fourier–Jacobi periods of $\mathrm{Sp}_4$ in new ramified cases and use this to formulate an explicit conjectural identity relating Petersson norms of degree 2 Siegel cusp forms and associated half-integral weight forms. We note consequences of our identity for the growth of Petersson norms, the size of Fourier coefficients, and non-vanishing of central $L$-values.
Problem

The refined Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture for Fourier–Jacobi periods of Sp_4 involves local integrals that had not been computed in certain ramified cases. These integrals are needed to relate Petersson norms of degree 2 Siegel cusp forms to the norms of their associated half-integral weight forms f_m.

Approach

The local integrals are computed in new ramified cases by combining a Cartan decomposition criterion with the Macdonald formula for spherical matrix coefficients. The archimedean factor is computed separately. Global theta series and adelic Fourier–Jacobi periods are then used to formulate an explicit identity. Part of the local calculations was formalized in Lean 4 with AI assistance, made public on GitHub, and registered in the Palomar Registry.

Results

An explicit conjectural identity expresses ⟨f_m,f_m⟩/⟨F,F⟩ as a sum of central L-values over newforms of level dividing m. Consequences follow for Petersson norm growth, Fourier coefficient sizes, and non-vanishing of central L-values. The key local calculations were machine-verified in Lean 4.
