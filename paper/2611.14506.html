Annular Counterexamples and Skew-Shape Block Identities

Adam Tilèpe

TL;DR
A companion Lean 4/Mathlib development formalizes the annular counterexamples, tableau-transfer matrix lemmas, and the skew-shape block-statistic identity, using only standard axioms.
Abstract
We establish four connected results on weighted order ideals, cluster coefficients, and block-statistic enumerators. First, we give a counterexample to Theorem 14 of Kang, Lee and Lim (European Journal of Combinatorics, 2026): in the rank-two annular cluster model, a single bridging-lamination coefficient row produces $1+q^2$, contradicting the asserted unimodality. We identify the unsupported initial case in their published induction. Second, we refute their log-concavity Conjecture 1: the same gap example violates log-concavity, and a further single-curve computation gives the full-support polynomial $1+2q+7q^2+10q^3+9q^4+4q^5+q^6$, which also fails log-concavity. Third, we establish the skew-shape block-statistic identity proposed in Kahane's Conjecture 4.4 (arXiv:2607.11225v1) for arbitrary finite skew Young diagrams, under an explicitly specified root-exclusion convention. Its proof combines commuting tableau-transfer matrices, a connected-block inverse kernel, and finite polynomial differential equations; the corresponding mathematical statements have Lean 4/Mathlib formalizations. Fourth, we derive a bivariate block refinement whose coefficients are nonnegative after shifting the order parameter by one and, when necessary, multiplying by a monomial in the weight parameter. We specify the correction to the printed root convention, confirmed by Kahane in correspondence, and the geometric sign and specialization conventions clarified by the relevant authors for the annular examples.
Problem

Kang, Lee and Lim asserted unimodality (Theorem 14) and conjectured log-concavity for single-lamination specialized cluster c-polynomials. Kahane conjectured a block-statistic identity for skew-shape cell posets, extending his fence-poset result.

Approach

Rank-two annular cluster models with bridging-lamination coefficient rows give explicit mutation polynomials that serve as counterexamples. For the skew-shape identity, commuting tableau-transfer matrices, a vertical-strip inverse kernel, and finite polynomial differential equations replace a matrix logarithm, under a root-exclusion convention confirmed by Kahane. All main statements are mapped to named declarations in a public Lean 4/Mathlib file.

Results

The polynomial 1+q^2 refutes the claimed unimodality, and both 1+q^2 and 1+2q+7q^2+10q^3+9q^4+4q^5+q^6 refute log-concavity. Kahane's Conjecture 4.4 is proved for all finite skew diagrams, together with a bivariate block refinement. The Lean proofs rely only on propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound.
