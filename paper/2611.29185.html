Area Stability for Largest Small Polygons

José Ayala Hoffmann, Joaquín López Gutiérrez

math.MG Nov 22, 2026 · v1 cs.CG
formalization verification geometry

TL;DR
Selected linear algebra results supporting the interval-arithmetic spectral verification (Neumann bound, inertia identities, Gershgorin criterion) are formalised in Lean 4 within the LSPectra artifact.
Abstract
We develop a second order calculus for area maximisation among small polygons of diameter at most one, culminating in rigorous computational proofs of stationarity, spectral stability and strict local maximality modulo orientation preserving Euclidean motions for the polygons $Q_n$, whose diameter graphs consist of a cycle on $n-1$ vertices and one pendant edge, for every even $n$ with $6\le n\le 64$. At a stationary configuration, positive multipliers force first order area decrease along feasible directions that shorten at least one active diameter. Along directions that preserve all active diameters to first order, the reduced Hessian governs second order area change modulo Euclidean motions. Positive multipliers, independent active constraint gradients, and a negative reduced spectrum imply strict local maximality. Outward rounded interval arithmetic establishes existence of strictly convex stationary configurations $Q_n$ with all $n-3$ reduced eigenvalues strictly negative. Together, these results yield explicit neighbourhood radii and rigorous quadratic area decrease bounds for all sufficiently nearby feasible polygons. The reduced spectrum also detects second order degeneracy and instability, as illustrated by an optimal quadrilateral and the regular hexagon.
Problem

Reinhardt showed the regular small n-gon is not area-optimal for even n≥6. Numerical candidates Q_n with Graham-type diameter graphs (a cycle on n-1 vertices plus one pendant edge) exist, but they lack rigorous proofs of local optimality.

Approach

A second order calculus for area maximisation under diameter constraints is developed: first-variation no-ascent criteria with positive multipliers, and a reduced Hessian on the active equality space taken modulo Euclidean motions. The LSPectra software uses outward rounded interval arithmetic on a gauge-fixed bordered system to enclose stationary configurations, multipliers and reduced eigenvalues. Supporting linear algebra lemmas are formalised in Lean 4. The interval arithmetic and the main stability estimate are not formalised.

Results

For every even n with 6≤n≤64, Q_n is proved to be a strictly convex stationary small polygon with all n-3 reduced eigenvalues strictly negative. This makes Q_n a strict local maximiser modulo orientation preserving Euclidean motions, with explicit neighbourhood radii and quadratic area decrease bounds. The quadrilateral and the regular hexagon illustrate degenerate and unstable cases.
