Problem

The goal is to classify the Gromov–Hausdorff space of nonempty compact metric spaces topologically, specifically by showing it is homeomorphic to the Hilbert space of real square-summable sequences.

Approach

The Lean development proves the implication from five explicitly stated literature inputs, taken as assumptions, to the homeomorphism with ℓ². The proof constructs invariant full-support probability assignments and finite-dimensional local models. It then establishes global AR domination and a discrete approximation property.

Results

The conditional homeomorphism between Gromov–Hausdorff space and ℓ² is formalized, and the registry machine-checked the proofs. The five literature inputs remain unproved assumptions in this snapshot.
