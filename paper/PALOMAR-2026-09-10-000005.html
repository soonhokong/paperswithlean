Abstract
A Lean 4 (mathlib) proof of the classification of 1-manifolds, specifically that every compact, connected, Hausdorff topological space charted on the half-line [0,∞) is homeomorphic to the circle S¹ or to the closed unit interval [0,1]. The proof follows David Gale's outline: shrink to a finite atlas of interval charts, then induct on the atlas size, merging two overlapping charts at each step; a connected overlap glues two charts into one (two boundary charts glue onto [0, 1]), while a disconnected overlap has exactly two components and yields the circle.

Problem

The classification of compact 1-manifolds states that every compact, connected, Hausdorff space charted on the half-line [0,∞) is homeomorphic to the circle S¹ or to the closed unit interval [0,1]. The goal is a machine-checked proof of this result.

Approach

The proof is written in Lean 4 on top of Mathlib and follows David Gale's outline. The atlas is first shrunk to a finite atlas of interval charts. The argument then inducts on atlas size, merging two overlapping charts at each step. A connected overlap glues two charts into one, with two boundary charts gluing onto [0,1]. A disconnected overlap has exactly two components and yields the circle.

Results

The registry entry gives a complete Lean formalization of the classification, and the registry machine-checked its proofs: each such space is homeomorphic to S¹ or to [0,1].
