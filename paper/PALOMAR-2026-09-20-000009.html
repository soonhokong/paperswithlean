A Lean 4 formalization of Mikko Fischer's explicit connected 24-vertex subcubic graph with independence number 9 and zero-forcing number 11. Problem

A proposed bound states that the zero-forcing number Z(G) is at most the independence number alpha(G) plus one for connected graphs of maximum degree at most 3. Mikko Fischer gave an explicit 24-vertex graph claimed to violate this bound.

Approach

The counterexample graph is encoded exactly in Lean 4. Its connectivity and subcubic degree condition are checked, and its independence number and zero-forcing number are both verified formally.

Results

The formalization confirms the graph is connected and subcubic, with independence number 9 and zero-forcing number 11. This yields a machine-checked existential counterexample to Z(G) <= alpha(G) + 1.
