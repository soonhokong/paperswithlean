Joint Approximation and the Mixed-Prime Character Group

Irina Yaroshevskaya

math.NT Nov 6, 2026 · v1 math.GN
formalization mathlib number-theory topology
Read PDF arXiv abstract Code
TL;DR
The joint approximation theorem and the one-prime character theorem are formalized in Lean 4 with Mathlib, conditional on an unformalized two-term logarithmic separation hypothesis.
Abstract
For the integer metric defined by the square denominators of two distinct primes, we prove simultaneous approximation to zero in one prime metric and to any prescribed integer in the other. A normalized convolution argument gives a uniform central spectral bound. Random relative translations of smooth weights produce an exact positive cas cade; Fourier positivity places every normalized finer convolution below its value at zero. A far-field recursion, the growth of merged square powers, and a two-term logarithmic-form estimate give summable fac tor bounds. Consequently, for every fixed pair of distinct primes p,q, the mixed continuous character group is Z[1/(pq)]/Z: no irrational pa rameter is continuous. The mixed completion is the product of the one-prime completions. For each fixed pair this also gives a compati ble rational join and a strictly finer compatible join of the associated real TVG topologies, with the same prescribed real character group. Exact witness search terminates at every strict precision and integer target. We retain the structural, density, divisibility, sequence and finite-certificate results leading to this proof. The existence argument does not give practical witness-size or runtime bounds, and it does not assume independence or equidistribution of the two digit systems. Se lected abstract components have been checked using Lean and Mathlib; the complete analytic argument has not been formalized. The corre sponding problem for three or more primes remains separate.
Problem

Determine the group of continuous characters of the integers under the metric built from squared prime-power denominators for two distinct primes. The question comes from earlier work on topologies generated by rational-valued Hamel functions.

Approach

A normalized convolution argument gives a uniform central spectral bound. Random relative translations of smooth weights produce a positive cascade, and Fourier positivity transfers the bound to joint approximation. A far-field recursion, the growth of merged square powers, and a two-term logarithmic-form estimate (from Matveev's bound) give summable factor bounds. Key components are machine-checked in Lean/Mathlib, with the separation estimate taken as an explicit hypothesis.

Results

For every pair of distinct primes p,q the mixed continuous character group is Z[1/(pq)]/Z, so no irrational parameter is continuous, and the mixed completion is the product of the one-prime completions. The Lean formalization covers joint approximation conditionally on the separation estimate and proves the one-prime theorem; several other parts of the paper are not formalized.
