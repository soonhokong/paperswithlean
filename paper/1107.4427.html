$δ$-derivations of n-ary algebras

Ivan Kaygorodov

math.RA Jul 22, 2011 · v2 math.QA
formalization algebra
Read PDF arXiv abstract
TL;DR

Abstract
We give a description of $δ$-derivations of $(n+1)$-dimensional $n$-ary Filippov algebras and, as a consequence, of simple finite-dimensional Filippov algebras over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero. We also give new examples of non-trivial $δ$-derivations of Filippov algebras and show that there are no non-trivial $δ$-derivations of the simple ternary Malcev algebra $M_8$.
Problem

The paper describes δ-derivations of (n+1)-dimensional n-ary Filippov algebras and of simple finite-dimensional Filippov algebras over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero. It also determines whether the simple ternary Malcev algebra M_8 has non-trivial δ-derivations.

Approach

Filippov's classification of (n+1)-dimensional n-ary Filippov algebras into n+4 families is used to compute δ-derivations case by case in explicit bases. For M_8, the algebra is built on an 8-dimensional composition algebra, and its known derivation structure and grading are used to analyze δ-derivations. A corrected version of the results was verified in Lean.

Results

The δ-derivations of all (n+1)-dimensional n-ary Filippov algebras are described, which also covers simple finite-dimensional Filippov algebras. New examples of non-trivial δ-derivations are given, and M_8 is shown to have no non-trivial δ-derivations. Compared with the published version, Lemmas 1–5 and Theorem 6 have changed statements and the proof of Theorem 7 is completed.
