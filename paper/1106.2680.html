$δ$-Superderivations of semisimple finite-dimensional Jordan superalgebras

Ivan Kaygorodov

math.RA Jun 14, 2011 · v2 math.QA

Abstract
In the paper, a complete description of the $δ$-derivations and the $δ$-superderivations of semisimple finite-dimensional Jordan superalgebras over an algebraically closed field of characteristic $p\neq2$ is given. In particular, new examples of nontrivial $\frac{1}{2}$-derivations and odd $\frac{1}{2}$-superderivations are given that are not operators of right multiplication by an element of the superalgebra.
Problem

The goal is a complete description of the δ-derivations and δ-superderivations of semisimple finite-dimensional Jordan superalgebras over an algebraically closed field of characteristic not equal to 2. This includes the nonunital simple cases K_9 and V_{1/2}(Z,D) in positive characteristic.

Approach

Simple nonunital superalgebras are handled by direct computation on multiplication tables, together with prior results on Jordan algebras of bilinear forms. Lemmas on zero nondivisors show that δ-(super)derivations preserve direct summands, which reduces the semisimple case to the simple components. A revised arXiv version corrects statements and proofs of the published version, and a title-page note says this corrected version was verified in Lean.

Results

The δ-derivations and δ-superderivations are fully classified. In characteristic zero, semisimple finite-dimensional Jordan superalgebras have no nontrivial ones. New nontrivial 1/2-derivations and odd 1/2-superderivations that are not right multiplications are exhibited, and J(V,f) in characteristic 3 is added to the list of exceptions.
