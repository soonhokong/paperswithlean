Abstract
A Lean formalization of the coarse-graining approach to quantitative stochastic homogenization of divergence-form elliptic equations: function-space, deterministic, probabilistic, and homogenization-scale theory up to the quenched minimal-scale theorem. The public main result is a uniformly elliptic, isotropic specialization of the quenched homogenization comparison estimate in Armstrong-Kuusi, "Renormalization Group and Elliptic Homogenization in High Contrast" (Inventiones Mathematicae, 2025), independently checked using Audit/QuenchedComparison/comparator.json, together with four further comparator-checked specializations under Audit/.

Problem

Quantitative stochastic homogenization of divergence-form elliptic equations relies on a long chain of function-space, deterministic and probabilistic estimates. The goal is to machine-check this theory, including results from Armstrong-Kuusi (Inventiones 2025).

Approach

The coarse-graining approach is developed in Lean in layers: function-space theory, deterministic estimates, probabilistic estimates, and homogenization-scale theory. The development runs up to the quenched minimal-scale theorem. The main statements are checked independently with comparator files under an Audit/ directory.

Results

The public main result is a uniformly elliptic, isotropic specialization of the quenched homogenization comparison estimate of Armstrong-Kuusi. Four further comparator-checked specializations are also provided.
