Long-horizon autoformalization of a core theorem underlying MIP* = RE

Sirui Lu, Ruixuan Deng, David Zhu, Zhengfeng Ji

quant-ph Sep 17, 2026 · v2 cs.AI cs.LO

TL;DR
AI agents under human supervision generated a 126k-line Lean 4 proof of quantum soundness of the low individual-degree test underlying MIP* = RE.
Abstract
Landmark mathematical formalizations have taken specialist teams years to complete. We present FormalFlow, a system that coordinates AI proving agents under human supervision to address statement drift and proof composition in long-horizon formalization. Drawing on software engineering principles and practices, it uses a shared blueprint to guide nested planning, proving and review loops. Agents strengthen verification and review throughout formalization. We completed a machine-checked Lean 4 proof of the quantum soundness of the classical low individual-degree test, a core theorem underlying MIP* = RE. Developing the proof took 63 days; greater parallelism could further reduce this time. The final library contains 126,367 lines of Lean code, all generated by agents. The formalization corrects side conditions and intermediate errors while preserving the published final error bound under corrected assumptions. This work provides a verified foundation for quantum complexity and demonstrates a route to affordable verification of major research proofs by small teams.
Problem

Long-horizon autoformalization of research proofs suffers from statement drift and difficulty composing many agent-produced components into one complete proof. The target was the quantum soundness of the classical low individual-degree test, a core theorem in the proof of MIP* = RE.

Approach

FormalFlow coordinates AI proving agents through a shared GitHub repository, an interactive blueprint, continuous integration and code review, organized as nested planning, proving and review loops. Every paper-facing Lean declaration links to a blueprint node, and each blueprint node links to a claim in the paper, so statements can be checked claim by claim. Agents and human auditors added audits and validity gates to catch weakened statements and unproved obligations. Foundations missing from Mathlib were formalized, including SDP duality with complementary slackness, bipartite Naimark dilations, and state-dependent distances between quantum measurements.

Results

A complete machine-checked Lean 4 proof was produced in 63 days, totaling 126,367 lines of agent-generated code. It corrects side conditions (k ≥ 400md and 0 < k) and intermediate errors while preserving the published error bound. The result is registered in the Palomar Registry, and the reusable infrastructure is released as the oh-my-formalization template.
