Existence, relative Morse index and the number of positive multi-peak solutions to subcritical Hamiltonian systems

Qing Guo

Abstract
We construct positive multi-peak solutions of a singularly perturbed Hamiltonian elliptic system with a common uniformly positive potential $a$ and powers $p,q>1$ below the Sobolev critical hyperbola. The peaks concentrate at any prescribed set of $k$ distinct nondegenerate critical points $P_i$ of $a$. The Hessian of the action is strongly indefinite. A Schur complement gives exactly $kN$ eigenvalues near zero and their leading terms, which have order $\varepsilon^2$ and are determined by $D^2a(P_i)$. The solutions are nondegenerate. A scalar Schur reduction and localization of its inverse term give the relative Morse index $k+\sum_{i=1}^k\ind D^2a(P_i)$. Local Pohozaev identities give local uniqueness. A compactness argument then shows that, for $m$ prescribed nondegenerate critical points, the union of the fixed profile neighborhoods associated with their nonempty subsets contains exactly $2^m-1$ positive solutions. Using uniqueness of positive solutions of the limiting system up to translations, we obtain the same count in classes defined by nonvanishing in fixed balls and a scaled nonlinear mass bound that excludes an additional peak. We also give second-order expansions of the peak locations and rescaled profiles.
Problem

The paper studies positive multi-peak solutions of a singularly perturbed Hamiltonian elliptic system with a positive potential a and subcritical powers p, q. It asks for their existence, nondegeneracy, relative Morse index, local uniqueness, and exact number when peaks concentrate at prescribed nondegenerate critical points of a.

Approach

A Lyapunov–Schmidt-type construction with a projected inverse and Brouwer degree gives multi-peak solutions near prescribed critical points. A Schur complement analysis of the strongly indefinite Hessian locates the kN small eigenvalues. A scalar Schur reduction then computes the relative Morse index. Local Pohozaev identities and compactness arguments give local uniqueness and the solution count. A Lean 4 formalization of the statements and proofs is provided in an accompanying repository.

Results

The constructed solutions are nondegenerate, with relative Morse index k + Σ ind D²a(P_i). For m prescribed critical points, exactly 2^m − 1 positive solutions lie in the union of the fixed profile neighborhoods. The paper also gives second-order expansions of the peak locations and rescaled profiles.
