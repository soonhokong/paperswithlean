Maximality of the Došen–Petrić Boolean Category

Florian Lengyel

math.CT Nov 14, 2026 · v1 math.LO
formalization category-theory logic
Read PDF arXiv abstract Code
TL;DR
Formalizes in Lean 4 the maximality proofs for the Došen–Petrić zero-identity Boolean category, including Boolean Coherence and occurrence-substitution correspondence.
Abstract
We prove that the zero-identity Boolean category of Došen and Petrić, freely generated by a nonempty set of propositional variables, is maximal. Adjoining any equation between parallel morphisms that does not follow from the defining equations, and closing under the operations on morphisms and uniform formula substitution, collapses this category to a preorder. This disproves their conjecture that the category is not maximal. The proof uses Boolean Coherence to select a pair of variable occurrences on which the two morphisms differ. Composition with morphisms selecting these occurrences, uniform substitution and two isomorphisms yield equality of the product projections. We prove that any zero-identity Boolean category satisfying such an additional equation under every interpretation of the variables is a preorder. We also give a Lean formalization of these proofs and their dependencies. It includes Boolean Coherence and the correspondence between substitution on tagged occurrence sets and substitution on finite ordinals.
Problem

Došen and Petrić conjectured that their freely generated zero-identity Boolean category is not maximal. The question is whether adjoining any non-derivable equation between parallel morphisms collapses the category to a preorder.

Approach

Boolean Coherence is used to pick a pair of variable occurrences on which two distinct morphisms differ. Composition isolates this pair, giving an equation (K_A). Uniform substitution, double negation and two isomorphisms then yield equality of the product projections, which forces a preorder. A second proof uses the mix morphism. The proofs are formalized in Lean 4, with formulas and indexed arrow terms as inductive types and the category as a quotient by a derivability relation.

Results

The category is maximal, which disproves the Došen–Petrić conjecture. The Lean development checks the maximality theorems, Boolean Coherence, and the correspondence between substitution on tagged occurrence sets and substitution on finite ordinals.
