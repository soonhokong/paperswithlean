TL;DR
Formalizes in Lean with Mathlib a finite-simplex specialization of a reduction from Blackwell approachability to improper phi-regret, with machine-checked proofs.
Abstract
A Mathlib-only nonempty finite-simplex, algorithmic specialization of Theorem 4 in Dann, Mansour, Mohri, Schneider, and Sivan (COLT 2025). It realizes the tensor action space as a joint simplex, defines the source improper comparator by adding a mixed-constraint tensor of the canonical action marginal, proves that comparator escapes the action set, and proves exact finite-horizon equality between approachability loss and improper phi-regret under explicit causal strategy translations in both directions. It also proves that every joint action is a mixture of at most one rank-one tensor per constraint index.

Problem

Theorem 4 of Dann, Mansour, Mohri, Schneider, and Sivan (COLT 2025) gives a tight reduction from Blackwell approachability to improper phi-regret. The entry gives a machine-checked version of an algorithmic specialization of that result for nonempty finite simplices.

Approach

The development uses only Mathlib. It models the tensor action space as a joint simplex. It defines the source improper comparator by adding a mixed-constraint tensor of the canonical action marginal to it, and translates strategies causally in both directions between the approachability and regret settings.

Results

Lean proofs establish four facts: the comparator escapes the action set; approachability loss equals improper phi-regret exactly over any finite horizon; this holds under strategy translations in both directions; and every joint action is a mixture of at most one rank-one tensor per constraint index.
