Veech dichotomy for specific covering surface over Veech surface

Haoran Hu

TL;DR
The main theorem (Theorem 1, compact branch configurations implying unique ergodicity of minimal directions) is formalized in Lean 4 with Mathlib, provided in a code repository.
Abstract
It is known that Veech dichotomy, which states that minimal directions are uniquely ergodic, holds for Veech surface and covering surface branched at a single non-periodic connection point of Veech surface. We give a sufficient condition for Veech dichotomy holds for a finite translation cover of a Veech surface, and applies it on branched cover of square-tiled surfaces to construct examples that satisfy Veech dichotomy but aren't Veech surfaces.
Problem

Veech dichotomy (every minimal direction is uniquely ergodic) is known for Veech surfaces and for covers branched over a single non-periodic point. The open question is when it holds for finite translation covers of Veech surfaces branched over several points.

Approach

The paper gives a sufficient condition: the ordered tuple of branch values lies in a compact affine-invariant subset of the configuration space, which means its affine-orbit closure misses the fat diagonal. Under this condition, compact returns of the Teichmüller ray on the base carry over to the cover, and Masur's criterion then gives unique ergodicity. A torsion criterion on the period torus yields concrete examples. Theorem 1 is formalized in Lean 4 using Mathlib.

Results

Pairs of branch values whose period-torus differences are nonzero N-torsion produce covers satisfying the Veech dichotomy. This gives non-Veech examples, such as genus-four double covers of a three-square surface. For the regular double pentagon, the criterion holds exactly when both branch values are side midpoints.
