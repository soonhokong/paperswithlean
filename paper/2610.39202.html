On the median of the beta distribution in relation to the median of the gamma distribution

Richard F. Lyon

math.ST Oct 29, 2026 · v2
formalization mathlib probability
Read PDF arXiv abstract Code
TL;DR
Most theorems and lemmas on beta and gamma median bounds are machine-checked in Lean 4 with Mathlib, stated using Mathlib's gammaMeasure and betaMeasure.
Abstract
The median of the beta distribution has no closed form, but it is closely tied to the median of the gamma distribution. Kerman's approximation has the ratio form $g(α)/(g(α)+g(β))$ with $g(x) = x - 1/3$, a lower bound on the gamma median, and it is a lower bound on the beta median for $1/3 < α< β$. The ratio form built from the true gamma median is a tighter lower bound, asymptotically exact as $β\to \infty$. The ratio form built from the Wilson-Hilferty approximation to the gamma median, implicit in a classical approximation to the $F$ distribution but little known as an approximation to the beta median, is an upper bound on the line $α= 1$ and, along each ray $β= rα$ with $r > 1$, for large shape parameters. The ratio form that is exact on the lines $α= 1$ and $β= 1$, $(2^{1/β} - 1)/(2^{1/α} + 2^{1/β} - 2)$, is numerically within 0.3% of the median when both shape parameters are at least 1, and provably less than 25% above it for $α\le β$; it yields a new approximation to the gamma median, which is an upper bound on it for shape parameter below 1 and a lower bound above 1. Interpolating between it and a ratio form with a $1/x$ correction gives, with no fitted constant, an approximation within 0.016% of the median when both shape parameters are at least 1. No ratio form gives the beta median exactly. For large shape parameters, the logit of the median has an explicit second-order expansion. For small shape parameters, bounds in elementary functions are asymptotically tight. An approximation in elementary functions that joins the two ends, with four fitted constants, is exact on the lines $α= 1$, $β= 1$, and $α= β$, and numerically within 0.065% of the median over the whole domain. Most of the proofs are machine-verified in Lean 4.
Problem

The median of the beta distribution has no closed form. Existing approximations such as Kerman's lack proven bounding behavior and accuracy guarantees across the full range of shape parameters.

Approach

Studies ratio-form approximations g(α)/(g(α)+g(β)), where g is built from gamma-median bounds and approximations. Analyzes asymptotics for large and small shape parameters and combines them into a domain-wide elementary-function approximation. Proofs are formalized in Lean 4 with Mathlib, using Mathlib's beta and gamma measures and CDFs; numerical checks use SciPy and mpmath.

Results

The ratio form using the true gamma median is a tight lower bound on the beta median and asymptotically exact as β→∞. A new gamma median approximation, log2/(2^{1/α}−1), is proved to be an upper bound below α=1 and a lower bound above it. A combined approximation is within 0.065% of the median over the whole domain, and most proofs are machine-checked in Lean.
