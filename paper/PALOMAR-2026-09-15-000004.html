Abstract
Lean formalization of selected results in B. C. Carlson's theory of special functions as complex Dirichlet averages. The registry selection establishes joint entire-parameter continuation of holomorphic averages on convex open scalar domains; joint holomorphy and native-integral agreement for the Gamma-regularized R and L functions on principal slit-node domains; Euler inversion and Euler-Poisson equations; and Gamma-regularized adaptations of both quadratic R transformations for all complex exponent and Dirichlet parameters with positive-real-part unsquared variables. The L function is related to R by exponent differentiation. The repository also supplies Pochhammer, several-complex-variable, simplex-integration, and Dirichlet foundations, which are not separately selected registry claims. This is a snapshot of an ongoing development, not complete coverage of Carlson's 1977 book or 1987 L-function article.

Problem

B. C. Carlson's theory expresses special functions of applied mathematics as complex Dirichlet averages. Its key results had not been machine-checked.

Approach

Selected results from Carlson's theory are formalized in Lean. The work covers joint entire-parameter continuation of holomorphic averages on convex open domains, and holomorphy of the Gamma-regularized R and L functions on principal slit-node domains, including agreement with native integrals. The L function is related to R by exponent differentiation. The repository also builds Pochhammer, several-complex-variable, simplex-integration, and Dirichlet foundations.

Results

The registry checked proofs of Euler inversion, the Euler-Poisson equations, and Gamma-regularized versions of both quadratic R transformations for all complex exponent and Dirichlet parameters. The development is an ongoing snapshot and does not fully cover Carlson's 1977 book or his 1987 L-function article.
