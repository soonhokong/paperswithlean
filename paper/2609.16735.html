Subspaces of $L^1$ spanned by the even and odd levels of the Haar system

Thomas Speckhofer

TL;DR
The proofs of the main theorem (the even-level Haar span is not isomorphic to L^1) and Corollary 3.4 were formalized in Lean 4 with Mathlib.
Abstract
We prove that the closed subspace of $L^1[0,1]$ spanned by the even (respectively, odd) levels of the Haar system is not isomorphic to $L^1[0,1]$. Moreover, the closed span of any infinite collection of Haar levels with infinite complement is isomorphic to that of the even levels.
Problem

Whether the closed subspace of L^1[0,1] spanned by the even (or odd) levels of the Haar system is isomorphic to L^1 was a long-standing open problem. Pełczyński's decomposition method does not apply because this subspace is uncomplemented in L^1.

Approach

The coefficient map from the even-level subspace X_0 into ℓ^2 is shown to be bounded with norm at most √2, using a telescoping inequality on L^1 masses of dyadic quarters. By Grothendieck's inequality, every bounded operator from L^1 to ℓ^2 is absolutely 2-summing, but this map is shown not to be, so X_0 cannot be isomorphic to L^1. A Haar-multiplier lemma and Pełczyński decomposition then show X_0 ≅ X_1 ≅ ℓ^1(X_0). The main theorem and a corollary were formalized in Lean 4 with Mathlib, with assistance from AI models.

Results

The spans of the even and of the odd Haar levels are not isomorphic to L^1. Any infinite collection of Haar levels with infinite complement spans a space isomorphic to the even-level span.
