A fast dynamo with zero topological entropy

Lukas Niebel

math.AP Aug 3, 2026 · v3 math-ph math.SP
formalization analysis physics
Read PDF arXiv abstract Code
TL;DR
The manuscript's fast-dynamo construction and proofs are formalised in Lean 4, with the formalisation released in a GitHub repository (FastDynamo).
Abstract
We construct an autonomous Lipschitz fast dynamo on the flat three-torus whose particle flow has zero topological entropy and whose ideal induction equation has no exponential growth. We perform a multiscale construction starting from the Ponomarenko flow. For every sufficiently small positive magnetic diffusivity, the induction operator on $\mathbb{T}^3$ has an eigenvalue with real part bounded below by a positive constant independent of the diffusivity. A corresponding non-zero real-valued magnetic field satisfies an exact exponential growth law in $\mathrm{L}^2$. For the same velocity field, every solution of the ideal problem grows at most linearly in $\mathrm{L}^2$. Moreover, the particle flow and its inverse have Lipschitz constants growing at most linearly in time and thus zero topological entropy. The velocity field is real-valued, divergence-free, differentiable everywhere and smooth away from one circle, but is not $\mathrm{C}^1$.
Problem

The fast dynamo problem asks whether a velocity field can sustain exponential magnetic field growth at a rate bounded below uniformly as magnetic diffusivity tends to zero. Known heuristics link fast dynamo action to chaotic particle flow with positive topological entropy.

Approach

A multiscale construction starts from the Ponomarenko flow. It places rescaled helical reference profiles in shrinking cells on the three-torus. The spectral problem is reduced to a horizontal operator on the two-torus. A two-resolvent approximate inverse and a contour-integral argument then establish an unstable eigenvalue, and the axial magnetic component is recovered from the divergence constraint. A Lean 4 formalisation of the manuscript is provided.

Results

The paper constructs an autonomous Lipschitz, divergence-free velocity field that is a fast dynamo. Its particle flow has Lipschitz constants growing at most linearly in time, giving zero topological entropy. Solutions of the ideal induction equation grow at most linearly in L2.
