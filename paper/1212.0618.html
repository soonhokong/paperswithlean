$δ$-derivations of semisimple finite-dimensional structurable algebras

Ivan Kaygorodov, Elizaveta Okhapkina

Abstract
In this paper, we show the absence of nontrivial $δ$-derivations of semisimple finite-dimensional structurable algebras over an algebraically closed field of characteristic not equal to $2,3,5$.
Problem

The paper asks whether semisimple finite-dimensional structurable algebras admit nontrivial δ-derivations. These are linear maps φ with φ(xy)=δ(φ(x)y+xφ(y)). The setting is an algebraically closed field of characteristic not 2, 3 or 5.

Approach

A direct calculation shows a nontrivial δ-derivation can only occur for δ=1/2, where it acts as multiplication by a fixed element. Lemmas then handle each type of simple structurable algebra in the classification, such as algebras of a Hermitian form. The semisimple case reduces to the simple components by showing a δ-derivation preserves each component. The proofs in this corrected version are stated to have been verified in Lean.

Results

Simple, and hence semisimple, finite-dimensional structurable algebras over such fields have no nontrivial δ-derivations. An appendix records that the proofs of Lemmas 1–4 and Theorems 5–6 were changed or corrected relative to the published version.
