Abstract
A Lean 4 formalization of manuscript Theorem 2.4: for every finite field K with |K| at least 4 and every m at least 1, a coordinatewise-affine strategy on the class of size |K|-1 cannot be completed to a winning strategy by arbitrary deterministic rules on the opposite class of size m, with respect to the fixed finite-field labeling.

Problem

In the hat guessing game on complete bipartite graphs, one question is whether strategies of restricted form on one side can be extended to a winning strategy for the whole graph. Manuscript Theorem 2.4 concerns coordinatewise-affine strategies over a finite field.

Approach

The theorem is stated and proved in Lean 4. For a finite field K with |K| ≥ 4 and any m ≥ 1, it considers a coordinatewise-affine strategy on a class of size |K|-1, under a fixed finite-field labeling of hat colors. It then quantifies over arbitrary deterministic rules on the opposite class of size m.

Results

The formalization proves that no such affine strategy can be completed to a winning strategy, whatever deterministic rules the other side uses. The Palomar registry machine-checked the proofs.
