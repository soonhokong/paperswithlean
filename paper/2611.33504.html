The Whitney sphere and tori as self-shrinkers

Jingyi Chen, Micah Warren

Abstract
We prove that the Whitney sphere in $\C^n$ is a self-shrinker for the Hamiltonian gradient flow of volume. We construct an explicit family of Lagrangian self-shrinking tori in $\C^2$ containing infinitely many pairwise noncongruent examples. We also characterize closed connected Lagrangian immersions whose Legendrian lifts lie in a Korányi sphere by showing that, up to scaling and a unitary transformation, they factor through the Whitney immersion via a diffeomorphism for $n\geq2$ and a finite covering for $n=1$.
Problem

Determine whether the Whitney sphere is a self-shrinker for the fourth-order Hamiltonian gradient flow of volume, construct Lagrangian self-shrinking tori, and characterize Lagrangian immersions whose Legendrian lifts lie in a Korányi sphere.

Approach

A shrinker criterion reduces the self-shrinker equation to the condition d div(JH) = 2c F*λ0, or equivalently to Δθ + 2cf being locally constant. This criterion is applied to the Whitney immersion and to an explicit family of tori. Maslov indices and Hamiltonian isotopy invariants distinguish the tori. All results are formally verified in Lean 4 using Mathlib.

Results

The Whitney sphere is a self-shrinker with constant 2n(n+2). There is an infinite family of pairwise noncongruent Lagrangian self-shrinking tori in C^2, lying in distinct deformation classes. A rigidity theorem shows that such immersions factor through the Whitney immersion, via a diffeomorphism for n ≥ 2 and a finite covering for n = 1.
