The Geometry of Banach space operator-valued measures with cyclic dilations

Daniel Freeman, Qianfeng Hu, Rui Liu, Jie Shen

Abstract
In this paper, we investigate the geometric properties of operator-valued measures with cyclic dilations. Under a localization condition on the normal part of the bidual, we establish James-type characterizations of shrinking, bounded completeness, and reflexivity for projection-valued measures. These characterizations apply to spaces admitting no continuous Schauder frame, including $L_1[0,1]$. Using vector measure representations and interpolation, we prove that an operator-valued measure admitting a cyclic dilation has a reflexive cyclic dilation if and only if the underlying space is reflexive. Under the same cyclicity assumption, we also characterize the existence of cyclic dilations on spaces containing no $\ell_1$ and on KB-spaces. The dilation spaces can be chosen to be order-continuous Köthe function spaces. Without assuming cyclicity, we give a weak compactness criterion and local variation conditions for reflexive dilations; an example on $\ell_2$ shows that reflexivity alone is insufficient. For a separable space containing no $c_0$, a normalized operator-valued measure is induced by a continuous Schauder frame if and only if it admits a cyclic dilation and has $σ$-finite operator-norm variation. Finally, for measure quotients whose synthesis maps admit bounded linear right inverses, we construct compatible cyclic $L_p$-dilations and represent the quotient spaces as inductive limits, with applications to ReLU Barron spaces.
Problem

The paper asks which geometric properties of Banach spaces, such as reflexivity, shrinking and bounded completeness, can be preserved when an operator-valued measure is dilated to a projection-valued measure with a cyclic vector.

Approach

Normal parts in the bidual are defined using the Yosida–Hewitt decomposition. Under a localization condition, this yields James-type characterizations for projection-valued measures. Vector measure representations, interpolation, and minimal alpha-dilations are used to transfer these results to operator-valued measures, continuous Schauder frames, and inductive limits. The stated results were formalized in Lean 4 and kernel-checked, with 70 textbook and literature results declared as axioms; the Lean code was generated with AI assistance.

Results

An operator-valued measure with a cyclic dilation has a reflexive cyclic dilation if and only if the underlying space is reflexive. Further results characterize cyclic dilations on spaces without ℓ1 and on KB-spaces, and characterize frame-induced measures on separable spaces without c0. Compatible cyclic L_p-dilations are constructed for measure quotients, with applications to ReLU Barron spaces.
