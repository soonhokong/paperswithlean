Completely regular growth of solutions to linear differential equations with exponential polynomial coefficients

Xing-Yu Li

Abstract
Consider the linear differential equation $f^{(n)}+A_{n-1}f^{(n-1)}+\cdots+A_0f=0$, where the coefficients are exponential polynomials. We prove that every finite-order transcendental entire solution has completely regular growth and obtain asymptotic formulas for $\log|f|$ on almost every ray. This answers a question of Heittokangas, Ishizaki, Tohge and Wen. The proof combines logarithmic derivative estimates with asymptotic integration along rays. We express the angular densities of zeros in terms of the indicator. We also extend the result beyond exponential polynomial coefficients under a sectorial Laurent–Puiseux condition.
Problem

The paper studies whether finite-order transcendental entire solutions of linear differential equations with exponential polynomial coefficients have completely regular growth. This answers a question of Heittokangas, Ishizaki, Tohge and Wen.

Approach

Gundersen's logarithmic derivative estimates are combined with asymptotic integration along rays. On each ray, the equation reduces to a dominant-frequency coefficient equation with an exponentially small error. Angular densities of zeros are expressed through the indicator, and the argument is extended to coefficients satisfying a sectorial Laurent–Puiseux condition. All numbered statements were formalized in Lean 4, with ChatGPT and Codex assisting development.
