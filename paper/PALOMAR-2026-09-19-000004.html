Abstract
A complete Lean 4 / Mathlib proof that the 7-adic zeta value ζ₇(3) is irrational. Here ζ₇(3) = L₇(3, ω⁻²) is the Kubota–Leopoldt value, equal to the 7-adic limit of the Euler-corrected Bernoulli numbers −(1 − 7^{w−1}) B_w / w over the weights w = 6·7^{n+1} − 2. The same is proved for η = ζ₇(3)/2. The proof is a determinant irrationality argument on the modular curve X₀(7), in four parts: independence of seven explicit germs attached to the weight −2 seven-adic Eisenstein series, with a uniform zero estimate; a seven-adic annular saving; an auxiliary-prime denominator estimate; and an Archimedean energy estimate with a kernel-checked numerical margin. It also uses an internal proof of the geometric continuation of the target series to every radius below 49, via a twisted U₇ matrix bound and initial overconvergence obtained from classical Eisenstein approximants. The Lean endpoints depend only on propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound. The mathematical argument has not been refereed. The work is human-directed, with extensive AI assistance in both the mathematics and the Lean development; see the automation section and PROVENANCE.md.

Problem

The goal is to establish that the Kubota–Leopoldt 7-adic zeta value ζ₇(3) = L₇(3, ω⁻²) is irrational, together with η = ζ₇(3)/2. The mathematical argument has not been refereed.

Approach

A determinant irrationality argument on the modular curve X₀(7) is formalized in Lean 4 with Mathlib. It has four parts: independence of seven explicit germs from the weight −2 seven-adic Eisenstein series with a uniform zero estimate, a 7-adic annular saving, an auxiliary-prime denominator estimate, and an Archimedean energy estimate with a kernel-checked numerical margin. Geometric continuation of the target series to every radius below 49 is proved internally via a twisted U₇ matrix bound and overconvergence from classical Eisenstein approximants.

Results

The Lean proof is complete and was machine-checked by the registry; its endpoints depend only on propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound. The work was human-directed with extensive AI assistance in both the mathematics and the Lean development.
