Perron–Wright asymptotics and critical component processes for inhomogeneous random graphs

Wentao Hu, Wen Sun

TL;DR

Abstract
We extend Wright's enumeration of connected labelled graphs with fixed surplus to irreducible critical finite type kernels and continuous compact type kernels satisfying a positivity condition. Let $\mathsf{C}_t(k,r)$ denote the total weight of connected labelled graphs with $k$ vertices and surplus $r$. Let $u>0$ satisfy $Ku=u$ and $\int u dμ=1$, and put $α=\int u^2 dμ$ and $β=\int u^3 dμ$. We prove that the rooted-tree radius $ρ$ is positive and real analytic near $1$, with $ρ(1)=1$ and $\logρ(t)=\frac{α^2}{2β}(t-1)^2+O(|t-1|^3)$, and that, for fixed $r\in\mathbb{Z}_{\ge0}$ and $0\le L<\infty$, $\mathsf{C}_t(k,r)=w_rβ^{(r-1)/2}e^{-k}ρ(t)^{-k}k^{k+3r/2-2}\{1+O_{r,L}(k^{-1/2})\}$, uniformly for $|t-1|\le Lk^{-1/2}$, where $w_r$ is the classical Wright constant. At criticality, the leading constant depends on the kernel only through $β$, under the stated normalization of $u$. At $t_n=1+λn^{-1/3}$, exact component identities combine with these asymptotics to give fixed size factorial densities and local size and surplus point-process limits for deterministic finite type models and annealed compact type Bollobás-Janson-Riordan models. With sizes rescaled by $β^{1/3}n^{-2/3}$, the limit is the Aldous process at parameter $αβ^{-2/3}λ$, and the corresponding type profiles concentrate at the Perron law $p(dx)=u(x)μ(dx)$. For every bounded uniformly positive countable critical kernel, we construct graph sequences with growing finite supports and the same local limits, and identify the additive shift of the critical window caused by spectral perturbations of order $n^{-1/3}$.
Problem

Wright's enumeration of connected labelled graphs with fixed surplus underlies the critical Erdős–Rényi graph and Aldous's limit process. It was unknown what replaces the Wright enumerator and its amplitude for critical inhomogeneous random graphs with Perron-critical kernels.

Approach

The work extends Wright's enumeration to irreducible critical finite-type kernels and to continuous compact-type kernels, using the Perron eigenfunction u of the kernel operator. Holomorphic implicit-function and spectral arguments analyse the rooted-tree radius. Exact component-count identities are combined with these asymptotics to obtain factorial moment bounds and point-process limits. Countable-type kernels are handled through finite truncations assembled into diagonal graph sequences. The main theorems are reported as formalized and checked in Lean 4.

Results

The weight of connected graphs with k vertices and surplus r is w_r β^{(r-1)/2} e^{-k} ρ(t)^{-k} k^{k+3r/2-2}(1+O(k^{-1/2})), uniformly in a k^{-1/2} window around t=1. With sizes rescaled by β^{1/3}n^{-2/3}, component size and surplus processes converge to the Aldous process at parameter αβ^{-2/3}λ, and type profiles concentrate at the Perron law. The additive shift of the critical window caused by spectral perturbations of order n^{-1/3} is also identified.
