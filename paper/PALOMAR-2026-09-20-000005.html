Problem

SparseStack is a sparse random sketching scheme. Its main theorem bounds the probability that the sketch fails to preserve the Gram norm of a fixed real isometry. The goal is a fully verified proof of a revised version of that theorem with improved explicit constants.

Approach

The revised main theorem is formalized in Lean through a retained-count proof route. The object analyzed is the actual independent uniform signed-bucket sketch under the rounded prescription C=80, B=169/20. All physical channel estimates and an all-order scalar certificate are proved in Lean.

Results

For every fixed real isometry, the Gram-norm failure probability is at most delta. The same prescription gives sparsity s<26.35 q/eps and sketch dimension m<176.9(d+q)/eps^2, with all proofs machine-checked.
