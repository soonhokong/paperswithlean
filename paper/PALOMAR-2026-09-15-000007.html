Abstract
A Lean formalization of continuous-path Markov processes associated with conservative Feller transition semigroups on nonempty locally compact Polish spaces. A uniform Kolmogorov displacement-moment bound yields a unique measurable kernel from each starting point to continuous-path space, with the prescribed finite-dimensional distributions. The library also develops strong Markov properties, generators and resolvents, stopping and killing, and Brownian motion as an example. The existence and uniqueness theorem is independently checked using Audit/ContinuousMarkovProcess/comparator.json.

Problem

Continuous-path Markov processes are usually built from Feller transition semigroups on locally compact Polish spaces. Constructing them requires existence and uniqueness of path-space laws with prescribed finite-dimensional distributions. The aim is to machine-check this construction and its surrounding theory.

Approach

The library is written in Lean. It uses a uniform Kolmogorov displacement-moment bound to obtain a measurable kernel from each starting point to continuous-path space. It then develops strong Markov properties, generators and resolvents, and stopping and killing. Brownian motion is treated as an example.

Results

The formalization proves existence and uniqueness of a measurable kernel to continuous-path space with the prescribed finite-dimensional distributions. The main theorem is independently checked via a comparator audit file, and the registry machine-checked the proofs.
