Problem

The target is a quantitative partial result on the Collatz conjecture. For every fixed c > 3/log(4/3), a positive proportion (in lower natural density) of positive integers reach 1 within c log x ordinary Collatz steps.

Approach

A Lean 4 formalization of eighteen selected Phase 1 statements, whose proofs were machine-checked by the Palomar registry. Besides the headline density bound, it covers fixed-target hitting, an exact target criterion, individual-root consequences, vanishing-clock-loss variants, depth-eleven density bounds, exact finite fan allocation, real-moment comparison, target profiles, and bounded reference entropy with explicit tail estimates. Density witnesses and levels are stated existentially.

Results

The eighteen statements, including the positive lower density constant valid for all c > 3/log(4/3), are formally verified. Several items are outside the formalized scope: full optimized formulas, complementary capacity, the Renyi-order right limit, amplified roots, and convergence of every positive integer.
