Abstract
A Lean 4 / Mathlib development of the second paper on the sets Z_{p/q}(s, s+t) = {ξ > 0 : {ξ(p/q)ⁿ} ∈ [s, s+t) for all n ≥ 0}, the generalized Z-numbers of Flatto, Lagarias and Pollington. The first paper built a corpus of block certificates for individual statements Z_{p/q}(U) = ∅. This paper replaces parts of that corpus by theorems, and then asks what the certificate format can and cannot reach. Thirty certificates in the regime p > q² collapse into one closed-form criterion on the single real parameter ε = {(p−q)s}, settling a set of positions of measure 1 − 2q²/(p(p+q)) at every base and for every real ξ ≠ 0; the criterion extends to a family indexed by a depth K, identified with the finiteness criterion of [FLP95] and with the parameter intervals J¹_b of [Bug04], the depth playing the role of the denominator of the rotation number. An explicit escape time for L ≤ ξ ≤ X is extracted from the soundness proof, and every unranked certificate of [Ste26] is made effective by it. A completeness theorem says the verdict is forced on the class where a certificate can exist — a finite hold set — and that an infinite hold set refuses every unranked certificate at every depth and over every denominator, which turns a bounded computer search into a theorem. A measure-theoretic depth–size bound 1 ≤ 2δ(K + 2 + log_{p/q} 2B) prices [KK17, Prob. 6.1] for this format. Finally a rank-stratified certificate gives ‖ξ(3/2)ⁿ‖ < 2/7 = 0.285714… infinitely often for every ξ ≠ 0, past the 31/81 = 0.38271… of [Dub19] as a window and past the corpus's own 1/3 as a nearest-integer constant.

Problem

The sets Z_{p/q}(s,s+t) are the reals ξ>0 whose fractional parts {ξ(p/q)ⁿ} stay in a window [s,s+t) for all n. These generalize the Z-numbers of Flatto, Lagarias and Pollington. Earlier work proved emptiness of such sets through individual block certificates, and it was unclear what that certificate format can and cannot reach.

Approach

Thirty certificates in the regime p>q² are replaced by a single closed-form criterion on ε={(p−q)s}. The criterion is extended to a depth-K family and identified with the known criteria of FLP95 and Bug04. An explicit escape time is extracted from the soundness proof, and a completeness theorem characterizes the cases where certificates can exist. The development is carried out in Lean 4 with Mathlib, and the proofs were machine-checked by the Palomar registry.

Results

The criterion settles a set of positions of measure 1 − 2q²/(p(p+q)) at every base. An infinite hold set rules out every unranked certificate, which turns a bounded search into a theorem. A depth–size bound is proved. A rank-stratified certificate shows ‖ξ(3/2)ⁿ‖ < 2/7 infinitely often for every ξ≠0, improving Dubickas's 31/81 window and the earlier 1/3 constant.
