Problem

Whether an o-minimal expansion of the real field can define a function that grows faster than every finite iterate of the exponential function.

Approach

The real field is expanded by the function x ↦ A(1+x^2), where A is a normalized analytic Abel function for e^x − 1. The resulting structure is shown to be o-minimal. The proofs are formalized in Lean and machine-checked by the Palomar registry.

Results

The expanded structure is o-minimal and defines a transexponential function, one that eventually exceeds every finite iterate of exp.
