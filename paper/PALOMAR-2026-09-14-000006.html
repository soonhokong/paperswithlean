Abstract
A Lean 4 formalization of the MI-32 upper bound, that is, of the Latała-Świątkowski conjecture that for every regularity parameter alpha >= 1 there is a constant C > 0, independent of the dimension and of the entry laws, with E||X|| <= C (M(X) + D(X)) for every square random matrix X with independent mean-zero real entries whose absolute moments satisfy ||X_ij||_{L_2r} <= alpha ||X_ij||_{L_r} at every real order r >= 1. Here ||.|| is the Euclidean operator norm, M(X) is the sum of the largest row and column standard deviations, and D(X) is the max-min weak moment at order log(k+1) taken over one shared deterministic deletion set on both axes. The probability space is arbitrary and the subunit order log 2 at k = 1 is kept literally. The selected theorem MI32.main_upper carries a complete proof whose transitive axiom audit reports only propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound. The proof runs through original-law positive-polynomial moments and interpolation, exact occupation-count localization, finite nets, a direct independent-coordinate Hilbert comparison, full original-law creator and annihilator contractions, positive vacuum iteration giving the local symmetric-law operator-moment theorem at the exact order log(n+1), a nested deterministic deletion decomposition with cubic two-sided variance screening, an elementary centered-Gram estimate for the far remainder, two two-shell block families controlled by the local theorem at scheduled orders, and the original-copy symmetrization reduction. Constants are explicit and conservative, not optimized.

Problem

The Latała-Świątkowski conjecture states that for every regularity parameter alpha >= 1 there is a dimension-free constant C with E||X|| <= C (M(X) + D(X)). The bound is claimed for square random matrices with independent mean-zero real entries satisfying the moment regularity condition ||X_ij||_{L_2r} <= alpha ||X_ij||_{L_r}. M(X) is the sum of the largest row and column standard deviations, and D(X) is a max-min weak moment over one shared deterministic deletion set.

Approach

The upper bound is formalized in Lean 4 over an arbitrary probability space, keeping the subunit order log 2 at k = 1 literally. The proof uses original-law positive-polynomial moments and interpolation, occupation-count localization, finite nets, and creator/annihilator contractions. Positive vacuum iteration gives a local operator-moment theorem at order log(n+1). The argument finishes with a nested deterministic deletion decomposition, a centered-Gram estimate for the far remainder, two-shell block families, and a symmetrization reduction.

Results

The theorem MI32.main_upper has a complete proof, machine-checked by the registry. Its transitive axiom audit reports only the standard axioms propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound. Constants are explicit and conservative but not optimized.
