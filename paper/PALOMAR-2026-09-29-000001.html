Adversary bounds and the polynomial method for quantum queries

TL;DR
Formalizes in Lean the negative-weight adversary bound, its duality and composition, and the polynomial method for quantum query complexity, checked against a Mathlib-only specification.
Abstract
Lean proofs of strong duality and exact block composition for the negative-weight adversary bound on total Boolean functions, explicit constant-factor characterizations of bounded-error quantum query complexity in value and Boolean XOR oracle models, and uniform extraction of query algorithms from supplied promise dual certificates. The submission also proves that every output probability of a q-query algorithm on Boolean inputs has a real polynomial representation of degree at most 2q in each model. Seven statements are compared against a standalone Mathlib-only specification of the finite quantum model.

Problem

Central results on quantum query complexity have not been machine-checked. These include the negative-weight adversary bound's strong duality and composition, its tight characterization of bounded-error query complexity, and the polynomial method's degree bound.

Approach

Lean proofs establish strong duality and exact block composition of the negative-weight adversary bound for total Boolean functions. They also give constant-factor characterizations of bounded-error quantum query complexity in both value and Boolean XOR oracle models, and uniformly extract query algorithms from supplied promise dual certificates. Seven statements are compared against a standalone Mathlib-only specification of the finite quantum model.

Results

The formalization proves the adversary-bound duality, composition, and characterization results. It also proves that every output probability of a q-query algorithm on Boolean inputs is a real polynomial of degree at most 2q in each oracle model. The registry machine-checked the proofs.
