The Wilson-loop formulation of Yang-Mills at finite mode truncation: a Level-Two structural bypass of the Gribov ambiguity

Yu. N. Berdinsky

hep-th Nov 24, 2026 · v1 math-ph
formalization mathlib physics
Read PDF arXiv abstract
TL;DR
Nine theorems on the Wilson-loop formulation of truncated Yang-Mills are machine-checked in Lean 4 with Mathlib, with no sorry, admit or new axioms.
Abstract
The unfixed real-time Henstock-Kurzweil functional integral for truncated Yang-Mills removes the choice of a gauge surface, but gauge orbits remain objects of the formulation: that is a Level-One bypass of the Gribov ambiguity. This paper carries out a Level-Two bypass at finite mode truncation: the Wilson loops W(C) are taken as the fundamental variables, so that the configuration space is a space of loop data and no gauge field, gauge orbit, gauge-fixing condition or Faddeev-Popov determinant occurs anywhere in the formulation. Nine statements are proved and machine checked in Lean 4 without sorry, without admit and without new axioms: the loop algebra is commutative, conjugation-closed and gauge invariant, with bounded generators and the SU(2) Mandelstam identity; the truncated Yang-Mills action is an affine polynomial in the plaquette Wilson variables; loop-expressible observables are cylindrical; the oscillatory integrand is Henstock-Kurzweil integrable on every bounded loop-space box for every calibration, with the Lebesgue value; the integral is independent of gauge-fixing data and gauge copies are the same point of the loop formulation; for loop observables the Level-Two integral with respect to the push-forward measure coincides with the Level-One unfixed integral; the one-plaquette SU(2) check is proved in full, including the closed form 2J_1(beta)/beta; the SU(N) Mandelstam relations are derived in covariant trace form; and the finite-M integrals are projectively compatible, their limit existing as a linear functional, not a measure. No continuum limit, mass gap, confinement or Makeenko-Migdal equation is claimed; the Giles reconstruction theorem is cited only. The honest report records exactly where each proof stops.
Problem

The Gribov ambiguity blocks gauge fixing in non-Abelian Yang-Mills. An earlier unfixed Henstock-Kurzweil integral avoids choosing a gauge surface, but gauge orbits still appear as objects of that formulation. The goal is a formulation at finite mode truncation in which gauge fields and orbits do not appear at all.

Approach

Wilson loops on a finite periodic lattice are taken as the fundamental variables, so the configuration space consists of loop data. The authors prove nine statements: properties of the loop algebra, Mandelstam identities for SU(2) and SU(N), the action as an affine polynomial in plaquette variables, cylindricity of loop observables, HK integrability, independence from gauge-fixing data, agreement with the Level-One integral via push-forward measures, the one-plaquette closed form, and projective compatibility. All are formalized in Lean 4 using Mathlib.

Results

All nine statements are formalized across nine Lean files; an axiom audit of 138 declarations shows dependence only on propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound. This includes the one-plaquette SU(2) integral in closed form 2J_1(β)/β, proved via a series argument because Bessel functions are not in Mathlib. No continuum limit, mass gap or confinement is claimed.
