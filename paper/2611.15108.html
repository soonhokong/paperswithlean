Typed Knowledge over Constructive Institutions: A Proof-Relevant Type Theory for Verified Science

Abstract
Proof-assistant kernels with strict propositions—Lean 4, Agda's Prop, and Coq/Rocq's SProp—build proof irrelevance into conversion ($p \equiv q$ for $p, q : P$), equating all derivations of a theorem. While natural for pure mathematics, judgmental proof irrelevance breaks provenance in verified scientific stores, where predicates, refinements, and institutional maps must distinguish independent derivations for consilience and counterfactual retraction; with subsingleton elimination, it also breaks normalization and algorithmic transitivity. We present the type-theoretic and institutional foundations of Eigenius, a kernel for provenance-preserving scientific stores. First, we formulate a dependent kernel with cumulative universes (Prop, Set, $\mathsf{Type}_k$ for $k \ge 1$), impredicative Prop, inductive families with index-forced subsingleton elimination, and intensional identity types: conversion $\equiv$ is untyped orthogonal-CRS joinability, while proof irrelevance is a witnessed axiom ($\mathsf{irrel} : ΠP:\mathsf{Prop}.\,Πp,q:P.\,\mathsf{Id}\,P\,p\,q$). Second, coercion-free subsumption unifies cumulativity, $Π$-covariant subtyping, and ontology inclusions; its bidirectional fragments ($\mathcal{K}_0, \mathcal{K}_1$) satisfy subject reduction, match declarative typing, and compile to heuristic-free checkers ($\mathcal{A}_0, \mathcal{A}_1$) sound and complete relative to strong normalization. Third, we link external verifiers via constructive institutions (Adopt, Discharge, Relativize), internalize justification logic with Why-provenance $\mathsf{supp}(j,P)$, and type open-world knowledge graphs via TTR. Finally, we prove consistency in Grothendieck universes and mechanize the metatheory in Lean 4 with zero sorry.
Problem

Proof-assistant kernels with definitional proof irrelevance (Lean 4 Prop, Agda Prop, Coq SProp) equate all derivations of a proposition. This loses the provenance that verified scientific knowledge stores need. Combined with subsingleton elimination, it also breaks normalization and algorithmic transitivity.

Approach

The authors define Eigenius, a dependent kernel with cumulative universes, impredicative Prop, inductive families and intensional identity types. Conversion is untyped orthogonal-CRS joinability, and proof irrelevance is an explicit axiom rather than a conversion rule. Coercion-free subsumption unifies cumulativity, Π-subtyping and ontology inclusions. Its bidirectional fragments compile to heuristic-free checkers, and external verifiers are linked through constructive institutions with justification-logic provenance and typed knowledge graphs.

Results

Bidirectional fragments satisfy subject reduction and match declarative typing, and their checkers are sound and complete relative to strong normalization. Consistency is proved in a stratified model over Grothendieck universes, discharged unconditionally in Lean 4 for finite universe bounds. The metatheory is mechanized in a 119-file Lean 4 library (EigeniusTheory) with zero sorry.
