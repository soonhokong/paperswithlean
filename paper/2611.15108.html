Typed Knowledge over Constructive Institutions: A Proof-Relevant Type Theory for Verified Science

Hans-Martin Will

Abstract
Mathematical proof assistants such as Coq, Agda, and Lean 4 build proof irrelevance into definitional conversion ($p \equiv q$ for $p, q : P$ with $P : Prop$), erasing which derivation established a theorem. While natural for mathematics over fixed axioms, judgmental proof irrelevance is incompatible with verified scientific stores, which must inspect and compare independent derivations of a claim to assess consilience and compute counterfactual retractions when an assay, reagent, or model is invalidated. Moreover, combining definitional proof irrelevance with subsingleton elimination breaks normalization and makes algorithmic conversion non-transitive. We present the type-theoretic and institutional foundations of Eigenius, a verified knowledge substrate for science. First, we formulate a dependent kernel with cumulative universes ($Prop, Set, Type_k$), impredicative $Prop$, inductive families with index-forced subsingleton elimination, and intensional identity types, where definitional equality $\equiv$ is untyped joinability under an orthogonal CRS and proof irrelevance is a witnessed axiom ($irrel : ΠP{:}Prop.\,Πp,q{:}P.\,Id\,P\,p\,q$). Second, we unify universe cumulativity, $Π$-covariant subtyping, and ontology class inclusions in a coercion-free subsumption judgment whose bidirectional fragments ($\mathcal{K}_0, \mathcal{K}_1$) satisfy subject reduction, compile to unification-free checkers ($\mathcal{A}_0, \mathcal{A}_1$), and match declarative typing. Third, we organize external verifiers into a three-level institutional hierarchy with explicit axiom policies (Adopt, Discharge, Relativize), internalize a justification logic with Why-provenance support $supp(j,P)$, and embed Cooper's Type Theory with Records (TTR) for open-world knowledge graphs. Finally, we prove consistency in Grothendieck universes and mechanize the metatheory in Lean 4 with zero sorry axioms.
Problem

Proof assistants such as Lean, Coq and Agda build definitional proof irrelevance into conversion. That erases which derivation established a claim, which conflicts with verified scientific knowledge stores that need to compare independent derivations and compute retractions. Definitional proof irrelevance combined with subsingleton elimination also breaks normalization and the transitivity of conversion.

Approach

The authors design the Eigenius dependent kernel. It has cumulative universes, impredicative Prop, inductive families, intensional identity types, untyped definitional equality via an orthogonal rewriting system, and proof irrelevance as a propositional axiom. They add a unified subsumption judgment with bidirectional checkable fragments, a three-level institutional hierarchy for external verifiers, a justification logic with provenance, and a record calculus for knowledge graphs. Consistency is proved through a stratified model in Grothendieck universes, and the metatheory is mechanized in Lean 4.

Results

Subject reduction, soundness of the unification-free checkers relative to declarative typing, and logical consistency are established. The Lean 4 development has about 23,000 lines across 38 files with zero sorry; consistency is discharged unconditionally in Lean for finite universe bounds.
