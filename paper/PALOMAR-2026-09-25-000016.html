Abstract
A Lean formalization of "Homogenization at a polynomial scale in high contrast" by Armstrong, Kuusi and Loher. The paper studies elliptic equations in random media whose conductivity can vary greatly. It proves that their large-scale behavior becomes predictable at a length bounded by a polynomial in the parameters measuring contrast and randomness, improving earlier bounds. The formalization includes quantitative convergence estimates, approximation by an effective constant-coefficient equation, and large-scale regularity results. It builds on the CoarseGraining library.

Problem

Elliptic equations in random media with highly varying conductivity behave predictably only beyond some homogenization length scale. Armstrong, Kuusi and Loher proved this scale is bounded polynomially in the contrast and randomness parameters, improving earlier bounds.

Approach

The paper's results are formalized in Lean, building on the existing CoarseGraining library. The formalization covers quantitative convergence estimates and approximation by an effective constant-coefficient equation. It also covers large-scale regularity results.
