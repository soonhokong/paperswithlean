Constructive families and an extension theorem for binary permutation X-rays

JD Jones

TL;DR
Lean formalization, machine-checked by the Palomar registry, of permutation realization results and an extension theorem for binary X-ray profiles.
Abstract
We prove permutation realization for every admissible binary X-ray profile whose rank deviations from the odd staircase lie in [-2, 2], at every order and without a bound on density slack. We also prove the one-sided families with all deviations at least -1 or all deviations at most 1, with at least 2^u realizations, where u counts positive or negative deviations respectively. For n >= 1, if a label multiset L is realizable at order n, then Q(L) = {3, 2n+1} multiset-union (L+2) is realizable at order n+2. If every source label lies in [2, 2n-2], then h(L) <= h(Q(L)), where h counts permutation realizations. Repeated source labels are allowed. A least-order counterexample to the unrestricted conjecture must have first deviation at least 3 or last deviation at most -3. The unrestricted conjecture remains open.

Problem

The question is which admissible binary X-ray profiles, described by rank deviations from the odd staircase, can be realized by permutations. The unrestricted realization conjecture is open.

Approach

Constructive families of realizations are built for profiles whose deviations are bounded. An extension map Q(L) = {3, 2n+1} ∪ (L+2) lifts a realizable label multiset from order n to order n+2 and is shown to preserve realizability. The proofs are formalized in Lean and machine-checked.

Results

Every admissible profile with deviations in [-2, 2] is realizable at every order. One-sided families (all deviations ≥ -1, or all ≤ 1) have at least 2^u realizations. Q does not decrease the realization count when all source labels lie in [2, 2n-2]. Any least-order counterexample must have first deviation ≥ 3 or last deviation ≤ -3.
