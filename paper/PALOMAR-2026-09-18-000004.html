TL;DR
Lean 4 formalization classifying the counts of good partitions, using kernel-checked exhaustive enumeration and stabilization arguments; refutes a published conjecture.
Abstract
A Lean 4 classification of the number of good partitions of an initial interval, where a good partition of {1, ..., n} is a set of pairwise disjoint nonempty blocks of at most three integers covering {1, ..., n}, each block summing to a power of three, and count n is the number of them. With N_u = {1, 2, 3, 4} union {3^t - 4, 3^t - 2, 3^t - 1, 3^t, 3^t + 1, 3^t + 2, 3^t + 3, 3^t + 5 : t >= 2} and E_2 = {13} union {3^t - 3 : t >= 2} union {3^t - 6 : t >= 3}, the four compared theorems say that for n >= 1 the count is 1 exactly on N_u, is 2 exactly on E_2, and is at least 3 elsewhere, and that the count is 2 at every 3^t - 6 with t >= 3. The source proves uniqueness for every n in N_u, records exactly two partitions at 3^t - 3 and at 13, and conjectures, immediately after that theorem, more than two at every other n, reporting a computer check up to n = 844; the family 3^t - 6 (first member 21) refutes that conjecture as printed, and the converse of the uniqueness statement is proved here. The lower bounds are three explicit partitions transported by four constructions each of which keeps a recoverable component; the exact values are exhaustive enumerations at twenty values n <= 86, kernel-checked and complete by a theorem about the search, carried to every power of three by stabilization above a threshold.

Problem

A good partition of {1, ..., n} splits it into disjoint blocks of at most three integers, each block summing to a power of three. The source work proved uniqueness on a set N_u, found exactly two partitions at 3^t - 3 and at 13, and conjectured more than two partitions for every other n, based on a computer check up to n = 844.

Approach

The Lean 4 development defines the count of good partitions and proves lower bounds from three explicit partitions. These are transported by four constructions, each of which preserves a recoverable component. Exact values are obtained by exhaustive enumeration at twenty values n <= 86. The enumerations are kernel-checked and shown complete by a theorem about the search, then extended to every power of three by a stabilization argument above a threshold.

Results

For n >= 1, the count is 1 exactly on N_u, 2 exactly on E_2 = {13} ∪ {3^t - 3 : t >= 2} ∪ {3^t - 6 : t >= 3}, and at least 3 elsewhere. The family 3^t - 6, starting at 21, refutes the source's conjecture as printed, and the converse of the uniqueness statement is also proved.
