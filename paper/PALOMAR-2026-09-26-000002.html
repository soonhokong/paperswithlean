Problem

The Myerson-Satterthwaite theorem says that when buyer and seller type supports overlap, no bilateral-trade mechanism can be ex post efficient (almost surely), Bayesian incentive compatible, interim individually rational and weakly ex ante budget balanced at the same time. The entry gives a machine-checked proof of the continuous-type version.

Approach

The result is formalized in Lean. The proof uses payoff equivalence and envelope identities to pin down expected payments under incentive compatibility. It then establishes a strict rent-gap identity B + C > S, which is incompatible with budget balance and individual rationality.
