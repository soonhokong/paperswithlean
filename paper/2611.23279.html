Existence of SRB measures for some Rössler systems

Théophile Caby

math.DS Oct 22, 2026 · v1

TL;DR
The analytic homoclinic-tangency criterion and an associated Taylor bound are formalised and machine-checked in Lean 4 with Mathlib; the numerical part is not.
Abstract
We prove that for the Rössler system $\dot x=-(y+z)$, $\dot y=x+ay$, $\dot z=b+z(x-c)$ with $a=b=0.2$, the set of parameters $c$ within $6\cdot10^{-5}$ of the classical value $c=5.7$ for which the flow has a Hénon-like strange attractor supporting a Sinai–Ruelle–Bowen (SRB) measure has positive Lebesgue measure. The key step, proved with computer assistance, is that at a parameter localised in an explicit interval within $5.5\cdot10^{-5}$ of $5.7$ the period-one saddle of the Poincaré map has a quadratic homoclinic tangency that unfolds generically; the conclusion then follows from the theorems of Mora–Viana, Benedicks–Young and Benedicks–Viana. The same method gives two further such tangencies near $5.7$ and one at the band-merging parameter $c\approx4.507$. The proof reduces the generic-tangency conditions to finitely many inequalities, through explicit $C^2$ graph-transform bounds for the local invariant manifolds of a Poincaré map whose area contraction is of order $10^{-11}$–$10^{-14}$, and a scalar tangency function. The inequalities are checked with interval arithmetic in CAPD and re-checked with an independent implementation based on the Kv library; the analytic tangency criterion is formalised in Lean 4. Whether an SRB measure exists at $c=5.7$ itself remains open.
Problem

Whether the Rössler system with a=b=0.2 near the classical parameter c=5.7 has strange attractors supporting SRB measures. Because the attractor is non-uniformly hyperbolic of Hénon type, cone-field methods do not apply.

Approach

A computer-assisted proof establishes a generically unfolding quadratic homoclinic tangency of the period-one flip saddle of the Poincaré map. The Mora–Viana and Benedicks–Young/Viana theorems then give SRB measures for a positive-measure set of nearby parameters. Explicit C^2 graph-transform bounds reduce the tangency conditions to finitely many inequalities, checked with interval arithmetic in CAPD and re-checked independently with the Kv library. The analytic tangency criterion (Lemma 4.5) and a Taylor bound are formalised in Lean 4 with Mathlib.

Results

The set of parameters c within 6·10^-5 of 5.7 for which the flow has a Hénon-like strange attractor with an SRB measure has positive Lebesgue measure. Two further tangencies near 5.7 and one at the band-merging parameter c≈4.507 are also established. Existence of an SRB measure at c=5.7 itself remains open.
