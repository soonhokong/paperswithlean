Higher-order estimates for $p$-harmonic functions in the plane

Juan J. Manfredi, Aelson Sobral, Eduardo V. Teixeira, José Miguel Urbano

TL;DR
Theorem 1.1, the uniform higher-order estimates, was formalized in Lean 4 with Mathlib using AI tools; the code is in a public GitHub repository.
Abstract
Closing a longstanding gap in the theory, we establish uniform interior $C^{k,β}$ estimates for planar $p$-harmonic functions with $1<p<2$. These estimates hold for every integer $k\ge2$ and every $0<β<1$ such that $k+β<s(p)$, where $s(p)$ is the optimal regularity order identified by Iwaniec and Manfredi. As consequences, we obtain decay estimates at critical points, a Liouville theorem with optimal growth exponent, and uniform higher-order estimates for the flux of planar $q$-harmonic functions with $q>2$.
Problem

Planar p-harmonic functions with 1<p<2 are known to have qualitative C^{m,α} regularity up to the optimal order s(p) of Iwaniec and Manfredi. Uniform interior bounds controlled only by a norm of the solution were missing.

Approach

Lewis's planar polynomial rigidity theorem shows that higher derivatives vanish at critical points. Combined with Schauder estimates at noncritical points and a compactness argument, this gives decay at one of finitely many scales, which is iterated to bound Taylor remainders. The main theorem was formalized in Lean 4 using Mathlib with AI assistance.

Results

Uniform interior C^{k,β} estimates hold for every integer k≥2 and 0<β<1 with k+β<s(p). Consequences include decay estimates at critical points, a Liouville theorem with optimal growth exponent, and uniform higher-order flux estimates for planar q-harmonic functions with q>2.
