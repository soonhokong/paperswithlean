A Lean 4 formalization of the relative consistency of ZFC + CH together with the following assertion. There is a countably saturated elementary extension of the natural-number arithmetic structure of cardinality aleph one; all such set-sized presentations are isomorphic; and no such presentation is ordinal definable. The class is defined without parameters. Removing CH from the asserted theory gives a consistent negative answer to MathOverflow question 382009 about definability up to isomorphism versus an ordinal-definable copy. Every notion in the final sentence is internal to the first-order model of set theory.

Problem

MathOverflow question 382009 asks whether a structure that is definable up to isomorphism must have an ordinal-definable copy. The case considered is countably saturated elementary extensions of the natural-number arithmetic structure of cardinality aleph one.

Approach

A Lean 4 formalization proves the relative consistency of ZFC + CH together with three assertions. Such a countably saturated extension exists. All set-sized presentations of it are isomorphic. No presentation is ordinal definable. The class of presentations is defined without parameters, and every notion in the final sentence is internal to the first-order model of set theory.

Results

The relative consistency statement is formalized, and the registry machine-checked the proofs. Dropping CH from the asserted theory gives a consistent negative answer to MathOverflow question 382009.
