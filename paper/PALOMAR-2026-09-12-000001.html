Problem

The Broughton–Huff theorem states that the ordinary union of a strictly increasing sequence of sigma-fields is never itself a sigma-field. The goal is a machine-checked proof of this result.

Approach

The theorem is formalized in Lean. The proof constructs a set that is measurable in the lattice supremum of the sequence of sigma-fields but belongs to none of the individual sigma-fields.

Results

A complete Lean formalization of the Broughton–Huff theorem, with proofs machine-checked by the Palomar registry. It also establishes the existence of a set that is measurable in the supremum but in no individual sigma-field.
