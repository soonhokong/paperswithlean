Sharp bounds on the Eliashberg critical temperature: a comparison principle, convexity in the spectrum, and the Kiessling-Altshuler-Yuzbashyan conjectures

Davis Rens

TL;DR

Abstract
In Eliashberg theory of phonon-mediated superconductivity with vanishing Coulomb pseudopotential, the critical temperature $T_c$ marks the linear instability of the normal state. Kiessling, Altshuler and Yuzbashyan (KAY) characterized it through the top eigenvalue of a compact operator on Matsubara-frequency sequences. They proved that $T_c$ is unique at strong coupling and conjectured $T_c<C(λ\langleω^2\rangle)^{1/2}$ for every coupling $λ$, with $\langleω^2\rangle$ the second moment of the normalized phonon spectrum and $C\approx0.1827$ sharp, a bound Leavens stated in 1977 on partly numerical grounds. A preprint by Kondratev, not yet peer reviewed, gives a proof of this bound and of all-coupling uniqueness. We give an independent proof, for every spectrum with finite second moment, based on a comparison principle that is also Kondratev's central step and was proved to first order by Bergmann and Rainer: strengthening the pairing interaction at every Matsubara transfer, strictly at the smallest one, strictly raises the top eigenvalue. At fixed $λ$ and $\langleω^2\rangle$ a single Einstein mode maximizes $T_c$. Convexity of the top eigenvalue in the spectrum gives sharp bounds on $T_c$ by the moments $\intω^nα^2F dω$, $0\le n\le1$, including Leavens's zeroth-moment bound $T_c\le c_0\intα^2F dω$, $c_0\approx0.231$ numerically, optimal at moderate coupling. All bounds persist under a Coulomb pseudopotential with symmetric cutoff. A prefix decomposition of non-increasing vectors proves a third KAY conjecture, not treated in the preprint. The finite-dimensional core, including that conjecture, is formalized in Lean 4, and two computer-assisted proofs independent of the comparison principle confirm the Einstein bound.
Problem

In Eliashberg theory with vanishing Coulomb pseudopotential, Kiessling, Altshuler and Yuzbashyan conjectured that T_c < C(λ⟨ω²⟩)^{1/2} with a sharp constant C≈0.1827 at every coupling. They also posed uniqueness and monotonicity conjectures about the top eigenvalue of a compact operator on Matsubara sequences.

Approach

A comparison principle is proved on a cone of non-negative gap functions with non-increasing Δ_n/ω_n: strengthening the pairing kernel at every transfer, and strictly at the smallest, strictly raises the top eigenvalue. Convexity of the eigenvalue in the spectrum gives moment bounds. A prefix decomposition of non-increasing vectors proves a third KAY conjecture. The finite-truncation results are formalized in Lean 4 (v4.35.0) with Mathlib, using only the standard axioms, and two independent computer-assisted certificates confirm the Einstein bound.

Results

The proofs establish uniqueness of T_c at every coupling, the sharp bound T_c < 0.182726…√(λ⟨ω²⟩), Einstein-mode optimality, and moment bounds including Leavens's zeroth-moment bound. These bounds persist under a Coulomb pseudopotential with symmetric cutoff. The finite-dimensional versions of these results, and Conjecture 3 in full, are machine-checked in Lean; the passage to infinite N and the T_c construction are not formalized.
