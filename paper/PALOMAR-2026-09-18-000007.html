TL;DR
Formalizes in Lean Theorem 1 on sharp bounds for Euclidean symmetry groups of real plane algebraic curves; proofs machine-checked by the Palomar registry.
Abstract
Let C be an infinite real plane algebraic curve defined by a polynomial of degree d >= 2 that is irreducible over the complex numbers, and suppose C is not a circle. Its group of Euclidean symmetries is finite, the orientation-preserving part is cyclic of order at most max(d, 2d - 4), and the full group has order at most 2d. Both bounds are attained in every degree d >= 2. For d >= 5, curves attaining the rotation bound are exactly, up to orientation-preserving similarity, the curves Re(z^(d-2)(|z|^2 + a)) = 0 with |a| = 1 and a not real; conversely each such curve with d - 2 >= 3 attains the bound. This entry formalizes Theorem 1 of the note; the note's second theorem (Mobius symmetries and genus of the extremal family) is not part of this entry.

Problem

The goal is to bound the Euclidean symmetry group of an infinite real plane algebraic curve of degree d >= 2 that is irreducible over the complex numbers and is not a circle. A further aim is to characterize the curves that attain the bound.

Approach

The registry entry is a Lean formalization of Theorem 1 of a note on symmetries of real plane curves. It covers finiteness of the symmetry group and the bounds on its rotation subgroup and on the full group. It also covers sharpness of these bounds and the classification of rotation-extremal curves for d >= 5. The note's second theorem, on Mobius symmetries and the genus of the extremal family, is not included.

Results

The orientation-preserving symmetries form a cyclic group of order at most max(d, 2d-4), and the full group has order at most 2d. Both bounds are attained in every degree d >= 2. For d >= 5, the curves attaining the rotation bound are exactly, up to orientation-preserving similarity, Re(z^(d-2)(|z|^2 + a)) = 0 with |a| = 1 and a not real.
