$δ$-derivations of classical Lie superalgebras

Ivan Kaygorodov

math.RA Oct 14, 2010 · v2
formalization algebra
Read PDF arXiv abstract
TL;DR
A corrected version machine-checks key lemmas in Lean 4, including Lemma 5, antiderivation computations for D(2,1;α) with symbolic α, and psl(2|2).
Abstract
We consider the $δ$-derivations of classical Lie superalgebras and prove that these superalgebras admit nonzero $δ$-derivations only when $δ= 0,1/2,1$. The structure of $1/2$-derivations for classical Lie superalgebras is completely determined.
Problem

The work determines which δ-derivations exist on classical Lie superalgebras over an algebraically closed field of characteristic 0. It also describes the structure of their 1/2-derivations.

Approach

The proofs use Kac's classification, root decompositions, and lemmas showing that δ-derivations preserve the even part. They reduce to the cases where the even part contains an A_1 or one-dimensional summand. The corrected version fixes errors in the published proofs, chiefly antiderivations of A_1 used in transposed form and gaps for B(1,n) and D(2,n). Several components are verified in Lean 4: Lemma 5, Lemma 16 for symbolic α in D(2,1;α), and the A(1,1) case.

Results

Classical Lie superalgebras admit nonzero δ-derivations only for δ = 0, 1/2, 1. The 1/2-derivations are completely described, and the main Theorem 22 is confirmed with a completed proof.
